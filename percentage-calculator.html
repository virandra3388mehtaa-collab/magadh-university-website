Percentage Calculator — अपने marks से % निकालें

Result aaya aur percentage ginna hai? Ya form me percentage bharni hai aur marks pata hain? Apne prapt ank (obtained marks) aur kul ank (total marks) daalein — percentage, division aur grade ek saath paayein।

Calculator

Percentage calculate karein

Sabhi subjects ke kul ank jodein — jaise 6 subject × 100 = 600।
Marksheet par diye gaye kul prapt ank bharein।
Jis percentage ke marks jaanne hain — jaise 75% ke liye 75 likhein।

Ye tool sirf aapki jaankari ke liye hai। Form ya official kaam me wahi percentage bharein jo aapki marksheet ya university ke niyamo ke hisaab se banti hai — alag-alag boards aur universities ke division ke niyam alag ho sakte hain।

Samjhein

Percentage kaise nikalte hain?

Percentage ka matlab hai — 100 me se kitna। Isiliye formula bahut seedha hai: prapt ank ko kul ank se bhaag dein aur 100 se guna kar dein।

Percentage (%) = (Prapt Ank ÷ Kul Ank) × 100

Udaharan: Kul ank 600 hain aur aapko 432 mile — to (432 ÷ 600) × 100 = 72%। Yahi hisaab upar wala calculator ek second me kar deta hai।

📊 Division (श्रेणी) kaise tay hoti hai?

Bharat ki zyadatar universities aur boards me percentage ke hisaab se division is tarah mani jaati hai:

PercentageDivision
60% ya zyadaFirst Division
45% – 60% se kamSecond Division
33% – 45% se kamThird Division
33% se kamFail

Ye ek aam guide hai — kuch universities me passing marks ya division ke niyam alag ho sakte hain, isliye antim faisla hamesha apni marksheet aur university ke niyamo se karein।

🎯 Target percentage ke liye kitne marks chahiye?

  • Pehle tay karein kitni percentage chahiye — jaise 75%।
  • Phir calculator me "Percentage se Marks" mode chunein, percentage aur kul ank daalein।
  • Udaharan: kul ank 600 aur target 75% ho, to 450 marks chahiye — (75 × 600) ÷ 100।
  • Exam se pehle ye hisaab karke apna lakshya (target) saaf rakhna sabse aasan tareeka hai।
Percentage ↔ CGPA

Percentage se CGPA kaise samjhein?

Percentage aur CGPA/SGPA ek hi baat nahi hain — aur inke beech koi ek universal formula nahi hai। Har board ya university apna alag conversion niyam rakhti hai। Jaise CBSE me CGPA ko 9.5 se guna karke percentage ka andaza lagaya jata hai (CGPA × 9.5), lekin ye niyam sirf CBSE ka hai — university ke liye nahi। Magadh University me SGPA/CGPA wahi mana jayega jo aapki marksheet par chhapa hai। SGPA kya hota hai aur kaise nikalta hai, ye is guide me samjhaya gaya hai, aur apna SGPA khud nikalne ke liye SGPA Calculator istemal karein।

Aksar Poochhe Sawaal

Percentage ko lekar aam sawaal

Percentage me decimal aaye to kitne point tak likhein?

Aam taur par 2 decimal tak likhna kaafi hota hai — jaise 72.45%। Form me jo format manga gaya ho, wahi apnayein; agar form sirf poora number (round figure) mange to wahi bharein jo marksheet par hai।

Kya percentage nikalte waqt fail hue subject ke marks bhi jodein?

Kul percentage ke liye aam taur par sabhi subjects ke kul ank aur prapt ank jode jaate hain। Lekin division ya eligibility ke liye kuch jagah sirf pass hue subjects gine jaate hain — isliye form ke niyam ya marksheet ki ganana hi antim mani jayegi।

60% aane par First Division pakki hai?

Zyadatar universities me haan — 60% ya usse zyada First Division mani jaati hai। Lekin kuch courses ya universities me niyam alag ho sakte hain, isliye apni marksheet par chhapi division hi official mani jayegi।

Percentage se marks nikalna kab kaam aata hai?

Jab aapko pata ho ki kitni percentage chahiye (jaise kisi form ki eligibility 50% ho) aur ye jan-na ho ki kul ank me se kitne marks lane honge — tab "Percentage se Marks" mode se turant pata chal jata hai।
