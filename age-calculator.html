Age Calculator — अपनी उम्र खुद गिनें

Sarkari naukri ya exam ka form bharne se pehle sabse bada sawaal — "kya meri age limit ke andar hai?" Apni date of birth daalein aur kisi bhi date par apni bilkul sahi age jaanein — saal, mahina aur din ke saath।

Form ke notification me di gayi cut-off date daalein। Aaj ki date pehle se bhari hui hai।

Ye tool sirf aapki jaankari ke liye hai। Kisi bhi bharti me age ki antim pushti usi ke official notification ke niyamo se hoti hai — form bharne se pehle notification zaroor padhein।

Samjhein

Sarkari form me age kaise gini jaati hai?

Har bharti ke notification me ek cut-off date di hoti hai — aapki age usi date par gini jaati hai, form bharne wale din par nahi। Age hamesha pure hue saalo (completed years) me dekhi jaati hai: jaise limit "18 se 27 saal" hai, to cut-off date tak aapke 18 saal pure hone chahiye aur 27 saal se ek din bhi zyada nahi hona chahiye। Isiliye upar wale calculator me dusri date wahi cut-off date daalna sabse sahi tareeka hai।

📌 Cut-off date kahan milegi?

  • Notification ke "Age Limit" wale hisse me saaf likhi hoti hai — jaise "Age ki ganana 01/01/2027 ke aadhar par hogi"।
  • Aksar ye date 1 January, 1 August ya form ki aakhri date ke aas-paas rakhi jaati hai — lekin har bharti ki apni date hoti hai, andaz mat lagayein।
  • Galti se form ki last date ko cut-off samajhna sabse aam bhool hai।

🎟️ Age me chhoot (Relaxation)

  • Sarkari niyamo ke anusar SC, ST, OBC, EWS aur kuch anya vargo ko upar wali age limit me chhoot milti hai।
  • Chhoot kitne saal ki hai, ye har notification me alag di hoti hai — wahi se confirm karein।
  • Chhoot ka fayda lene ke liye sahi caste/category certificate taiyaar rakhein — bina certificate ke chhoot nahi milti।
Aksar Poochhe Sawaal

Age ko lekar aam sawaal

Cut-off date tak 18 saal pure hone me kuch din kam hain — kya form bhar sakte hain?

Nahi। Minimum age cut-off date tak puri honi chahiye — ek din kam hone par bhi form reject ho sakta hai। Yahi haal maximum age ka hai: limit wale saal se ek din zyada bhi bahar kar sakta hai।

Form me kaun si date of birth maani jaati hai?

Lagbhag har sarkari bharti me Matric (10th) ke certificate par chhapi date of birth hi maani jaati hai। Agar Aadhaar ya kisi aur document me DOB alag hai, to form me certificate wali hi DOB bharein — aur ho sake to documents ki galti pehle sudharwa lein।

Age dinon me ginna kyun zaroori hai?

Kyuki limit saalo me hoti hai, lekin faisla dino par hota hai। Do ummidwaar "27 saal" ke ho sakte hain — ek cut-off se pehle birthday mana chuka hai (bahar), dusra abhi manayega (andar)। Calculator ka saal-mahina-din wala hisaab yahi saaf karta hai।

Kya ye calculator bilkul sahi age deta hai?

Haan — ye aam calendar ke hisaab se saal, mahina aur din ki exact ganana karta hai। Phir bhi form ki antim pushti hamesha official notification ke niyamo se hi karein।
