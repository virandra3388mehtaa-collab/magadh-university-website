About the University

A citadel of learning in the land of enlightenment

Magadh University was established on 1 March 1962 under the Magadh University Act, 1961, and began functioning the following day. It owes its founding to Satyendra Narayan Sinha, the then Education Minister of Bihar, while the foundation of the university was laid by Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, the then President of India. The renowned historian K. K. Dutta served as its founder Vice-Chancellor.

The university started with two constituent colleges, 32 affiliated colleges and seven postgraduate departments. It has since grown into the largest university of Bihar, and is today governed by the Bihar State University Act, 1976. Its jurisdiction spans the five districts of Gaya, Aurangabad, Nawada, Jehanabad and Arwal, and its secular, disciplined campus draws students from across India and abroad every year.

The Bodh Gaya Setting

Studying where the Buddha found enlightenment

The university stands at Bodh Gaya, a short distance from the Mahabodhi Temple and the Bodhi Tree under which Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment. Few universities in the world teach within sight of such a civilisational landmark — a setting that shapes the university's strength in Buddhist Studies, Pali & Prakrit, philosophy and the humanities, and gives its campus its distinctive, contemplative character.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions Magadh University students ask most — results, scholarship, colleges and admissions.

How can I check my Magadh University result?

Results are published on the official portal magadhonline.in. Open the portal, choose your session tab, click “Download Result” for your semester, and enter your Registration Number, Roll Number and the captcha code to view and download your marksheet.

How can I check my Bihar Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) status?

BC and EBC students check their status on pmsonline.bihar.gov.in and SC/ST students on scstpmsonline.bihar.gov.in. Log in or open “Application Status” and enter your Aadhaar or registration details and registered mobile number. Payment status can also be tracked on PFMS (pfms.nic.in).

How many colleges are under Magadh University?

Magadh University has 19 constituent colleges and 39 affiliated colleges across five districts of Bihar — Arwal, Aurangabad, Gaya, Jehanabad and Nawada — along with 22 postgraduate departments. See the Colleges section for the list.

What courses does Magadh University offer?

The university offers undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), professional, research (Ph.D.) and distance-learning programmes across the humanities, social sciences, sciences, commerce, law and professional studies. See the Programmes section to explore them.

How does admission to Magadh University work?

Admission to university departments and colleges follows the notifications published on the official portal each academic session. Watch the official website and the Latest Updates section of this page for admission notices, then apply within the announced dates.

Is this the official website of Magadh University?

No. This is an unofficial information website made for students. The official website of Magadh University is magadhuniversity.ac.in, and results are published only on magadhonline.in.
