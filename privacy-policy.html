Privacy Policy

Magadh University Info ek unofficial student-help website hai. Hamari koshish hai ki aapko saaf shabdon me pata ho ki is website par aapki kaunsi jaankari li jati hai aur uska kya hota hai. Is page ko padhna matlab aap is policy ko samajh kar istemal kar rahe hain.

Hum kaunsi jaankari lete hain

  • Is website par koi account, login ya registration nahi hai. Hum aapse naam, roll number, Aadhaar ya koi personal document kabhi nahi maangte.
  • Agar aap Student Help page ke form ya email se sampark karte hain, to jo jaankari aap khud bhejte hain (jaise naam aur email) wahi hamare paas aati hai. Iska istemal sirf aapke sawaal ka jawab dene ke liye hota hai.
  • Website ke server aur analytics tool apne aap kuch takneeki jaankari jodte hain — jaise kaunsa page khula, device aur browser ka type, aur lagbhag kshetra. Isse kisi vyakti ki pehchan nahi hoti.

Cookies aur analytics

  • Hum Google Analytics ka istemal karte hain taaki samajh sakein ki kaunse page students ke kaam ke hain. Google Analytics cookies ke zariye upar wali takneeki jaankari jodta hai.
  • Google AdSense ke vigyapan jab is site par chalenge, to Google aur uske partner cookies ka istemal karke aapki pichli visits ke aadhar par vigyapan dikha sakte hain. Aap chahein to apne browser ki settings se cookies band kar sakte hain, ya Google ke Ads Settings page par jaakar personalised ads band kar sakte hain.
  • Cookies band karne se website ki jaankari padhne me koi rukawat nahi aati.

Affiliate links

Is website par kuch kitaabon ke Amazon links hain. Ye affiliate links hain — agar aap unse kharidari karte hain to humein chhota sa commission mil sakta hai, aur aapko koi extra paisa nahi dena padta. Kitaab ki keemat aur jaankari Amazon par hi final hoti hai.

Doosri websites ke links

Hum Magadh University ki official website (magadhuniversity.ac.in), official portal (magadhonline.in) aur doosri sarkari websites ke links dete hain taaki aap seedha sahi jagah pahunchein. Un websites ki apni privacy policy hoti hai; wahan di gayi jaankari ke liye wahi website zimmedaar hai.
