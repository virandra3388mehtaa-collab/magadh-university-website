Unofficial website

Magadh University Info (magadhuniversityinfo.in) ek swatantra, unofficial student-help website hai. Ye Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ki official website nahi hai, aur na hi iska University se koi prashasanik ya vanijyik sambandh hai. University ki official website hai: magadhuniversity.ac.in.

Jaankari ki seema

  • Yahan di gayi jaankari official notices aur official website ke aadhar par, poori savdhani ke saath taiyaar ki jati hai. Phir bhi tareekhein, fees, niyam aur prakriya samay ke saath badal sakte hain.
  • Kisi bhi aavedan, fees ya pariksha se pehle official notice zaroor padhein. Sirf is website ki jaankari par nirbhar karke liye gaye faisle ki zimmedaari website ki nahi hogi.
  • Agar koi jaankari galat ya purani lage to Contact Us page se batayein — hum use jaanch kar sudharenge.

Fees aur seva

Ye website koi admission nahi deti, koi fees nahi leti aur koi certificate nahi banati. Agar koi vyakti is website ka naam lekar paise maange, to woh dhokha hai — kripya turant humein batayein aur paisa na dein.
