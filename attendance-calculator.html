Samjhein

Attendance percentage kaise nikalte hain?

Attendance percentage ka matlab hai — kul hui classes me se aap kitni me maujood the। Formula bahut seedha hai: attended classes ko kul classes se bhaag dein aur 100 se guna kar dein।

Attendance (%) = (Attended Classes ÷ Kul Classes) × 100

Udaharan: Kul 60 classes hui aur aap 45 me maujood the — to (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%। Yahi hisaab upar wala calculator ek second me kar deta hai।

📋 75% ka niyam kya hai?

Bharat ki zyadatar universities aur colleges me exam me baithne ke liye kam se kam 75% attendance manga jata hai। Iska matlab:

AttendanceMatlab
75% ya zyadaSafe — exam ke liye eligible
65% – 75% se kamDhyan dein — har class attend karein
65% se kamKhatra — turant college se sampark karein

Kuch colleges me bimari ya khaas wajah se condonation (chhoot) milti hai, lekin uske liye application aur documents (jaise medical certificate) lagte hain। Antim niyam wahi hoga jo aapke college ke notice me likha hai — isliye apne department se zaroor poochhein।

🎯 Attendance kam ho to kya karein?

  • Har class attend karein — aane wali har class percentage badhati hai, chhooti hui har class ghatati hai।
  • Upar calculator me "Target % ke liye aur kitni classes" mode se ginein ki lagatar kitni classes attend karni hain।
  • Udaharan: 60 me se 40 classes attend ki (66.7%) — 75% paane ke liye lagatar 20 aur classes attend karni hongi।
  • Agar bimari ya koi majboori thi, to medical certificate aur application ke saath HOD ya principal se baat karein — der na karein।
  • Apni attendance har hafte check karte rahein, exam ke aakhri din ka intezar na karein।
Aksar Poochhe Sawaal

Attendance ko lekar aam sawaal

75% se kam attendance ho to kya exam nahi de sakte?

Aam taur par haan — zyadatar colleges me 75% se kam attendance par exam form ya admit card rok diya jata hai। Lekin kuch colleges me medical ya khaas wajah se chhoot (condonation) milti hai। Apne college ke niyam aur notice hi antim honge, isliye department se turant baat karein।

Attendance percentage me practical aur theory classes alag gini jati hain?

Kuch colleges me theory aur practical ki attendance alag-alag gini jati hai, kuch me kul milakar। Apne college ke hisaab se alag-alag ginna ho to dono ke liye calculator alag se chalakar dekhein।

Ek class chhodne se kitni % attendance ghat-ti hai?

Ye kul classes par depend karta hai — jitni kam kul classes, utna zyada asar। Jaise 20 kul classes me 1 chhooti to 5% ghat-ti hai, lekin 100 kul classes me 1 chhooti to sirf 1%। Isiliye semester ke shuru me har class zyada keemti hoti hai।

Attendance badhane ka sabse tez tareeka kya hai?

Koi shortcut nahi — aane wali har class attend karna hi ekmatra tareeka hai। Upar wale calculator se ginein ki target tak pahunchne ke liye lagatar kitni classes chahiye, aur use apna daily target bana lein।
