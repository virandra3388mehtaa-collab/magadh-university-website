Magadh University Exam Form Me Subject Change Kaise Karein — Subject Badalne Ka Guide

Exam form bharte samay kabhi-kabhi galat subject ya paper chun liya jata hai — jaise honours paper ki jagah koi aur paper, ya subsidiary subject galat bhar diya gaya. Seedha jawab hai ki subject tab tak aasani se badla ja sakta hai jab tak exam form submit nahi hua hai; form submit hone ke baad subject badalna mushkil hota hai aur uske liye college ke exam section me application deni padti hai — sudhaar college ya university ki prakriya se hota hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki subject kab badla ja sakta hai, last date kya hoti hai, kaun se documents chahiye aur steps kya hain.

Exam Form Me Subject Badla Ja Sakta Hai?

Haan, exam form me subject badla ja sakta hai — lekin sabse aasan tareeka form submit karne se pehle hi use theek karna hai. Jab aap online exam form bhar rahe hote hain aur submit button dabane se pehle galti dikh jaye, to wapas jakar subject theek karke phir submit karein. Form ek baar submit ho jaye aur fee jama ho jaye, to portal par khud se badlaav karna aam taur par band ho jata hai — uske baad subject badalne ke liye college ke exam section me likhit application deni hoti hai. Kya sudhaar hoga aur kaise hoga, ye aapke semester ke exam notice aur college ki prakriya par nirbhar karta hai.

Subject Change Ki Last Date Kya Hai?

Subject change ki koi ek university-level last date is article me nahi likhi gayi hai, kyunki har semester ka exam form bharne ki khidki (window) alag hoti hai. Aam taur par subject tab tak badla ja sakta hai jab tak us semester ka exam form bharne ki aakhri tareekh nahi nikal jati — wahi tareekh subject sudhaar ki bhi aakhri tareekh hoti hai. Apne semester ke exam form wale official notice me last date zaroor dekhein (magadhuniversity.ac.in par notice section me) aur form bharte hi use check karein. Last date nikalne ke baad subject badalna bahut mushkil ho jata hai.

Kaun Subject Badal Sakta Hai?

Subject badalne ka matlab hai exam form me chune gaye paper ka sudhaar — jaise galat paper chun liya ho, ya subsidiary / generic subject galat bhar diya gaya ho. Honours subject badalna isse alag baat hai — honours subject admission ke samay tay hota hai, exam form se nahi badalta; admission ke baad honours badalne ki alag prakriya hoti hai, jo college se poochhni chahiye. Isi tarah, course badalna (jaise BA se BSc) bhi exam form ka kaam nahi hai. Exam form me sirf us semester ke papers ka chunav sudhara jata hai.

Subject Change Ke Liye Kaun Se Documents Chahiye?

College aam taur par ye documents maang sakta hai — bhara hua exam form ya uski receipt, registration card, aur sahi subject dikhane wala document, jaise admission receipt ya pichhle semester ki marksheet. Application me saaf likhein ki kaun sa subject galat bhara gaya hai aur kaun sa sahi hona chahiye. Agar aapke paas form bharte samay ka screenshot ya receipt hai, to use bhi saath rakhein — isse office wale aapki galti ko aasani se samajh sakte hain. Sahi documents ki list ke liye apne college se hi confirm karein — har college ki maang thodi alag ho sakti hai.

Step-by-Step: Exam Form Me Subject Kaise Badlein

Steps ye hain: (1) form submit karne se pehle subject ki list ek baar phir dhyaan se padhein — honours, subsidiary aur generic paper teeno check karein, (2) galti dikhte hi form submit karne se pehle use theek karein aur phir submit karein, (3) agar form submit ho chuka hai to turant apne college ke exam section me jakar likhit application dein aur galti batayein, (4) application ke saath exam form ki receipt aur registration card ki copy jodein, (5) sudhaar ke baad admit card aane par dobara check karein ki sahi subject chhapa hai ya nahi. Application dete samay apna registration number aur session bhi likhein, taaki office wale aapka record aasani se dhoondh sakein.

Subject Change Ki Fees Lagti Hai Kya?

Subject change ki fees ke baare me is article me koi rakam nahi likhi gayi hai — fees lagti hai ya nahi, ye aapke college ya university ke niyam par nirbhar karta hai, isliye apne college se hi poochhein. Form submit karne se pehle subject theek karne par aam taur par koi alag fees nahi lagti. Agar submit ke baad sudhaar ke liye fees mangi jaye to uski receipt zaroor lein. Koi bhi vyakti subject badalne ke naam par aapse paise mange to pehle college office se jaanch karein — official sudhaar hamesha college ya university ki prakriya se hota hai, kisi bicholiye se nahi.

Subject Na Badla To Kya Hoga?

Agar subject samay par nahi badla to admit card par wahi galat subject chhap jayega, jo form me bhara gaya tha — aur aapko usi paper ki pariksha deni pad sakti hai. Galat subject ka paper dene se aapka result bhi usi subject ke hisaab se banega, jisse aage chal kar pareshani ho sakti hai. Isiliye form bharte hi subject check karna aur admit card aate hi use phir se padhna sabse zaroori hai. Admit card par galat subject dikhe to turant college ko batayein — admit card sudhaar guide me iski poori prakriya batayi gayi hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Exam form submit karne ke baad subject badla ja sakta hai?
A: Aam taur par portal par khud se badlaav band ho jata hai, lekin college ke exam section me likhit application dekar sudhaar karwaya ja sakta hai — ye aapke semester ke exam notice aur college ki prakriya par nirbhar karta hai. Isliye galti dikhte hi turant college ko batayein.

Q2. Honours subject exam form se badla ja sakta hai?
A: Nahi — honours subject admission ke samay tay hota hai aur exam form se nahi badalta. Honours badalne ki prakriya admission se judi hoti hai, jo college se poochhni chahiye.

Q3. Subject change ki last date kaise pata karein?
A: Apne semester ke exam form wale official notice me last date dekhein — magadhuniversity.ac.in par notice section me notice milta hai. Aam taur par form bharne ki aakhri tareekh hi subject sudhaar ki bhi aakhri tareekh hoti hai.

Q4. Admit card par galat subject chhap gaya to kya karein?
A: Turant apne college ke exam section ko batayein aur exam form ki receipt saath le jayein. Admit card me galti sudhaar ki poori prakriya alag guide me batayi gayi hai — exam ke din ka intezaar mat karein.
