Magadh University Exam Me Absent Hone Par Kya Hota Hai — Poori Guide

Bimari, parivar me emergency, train chhoot jana ya kisi aur wajah se exam chhoot jana — yeh kisi bhi student ke saath ho sakta hai, aur us samay sabse bada sawal yahi hota hai ki ab kya hoga: kya saal kharab ho gaya, kya dobara exam de sakte hain, aur kya karna chahiye? Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki Magadh University ke exam me absent hone par kya hota hai, ek paper miss hone par aage ka rasta kya hai, practical chhootne par kya karein aur internal exam miss hone par kya asar padta hai. Is article me tareekhein sirf wahi likhi gayi hain jo university ki official jaankari ya is website par pehle se chhapi guide me di gayi hain — baaki baatein aam prakriya ki samajh ke liye hain, aur apne college se confirm karna hi aakhri hoga.

Ek Paper Me Absent Hone Par Kya Hota Hai?

Exam ke kisi ek paper me absent hone ka matlab hai ki us paper me aap fail mane jayenge — lekin iska matlab yeh nahi hai ki poora semester ya saal kharab ho gaya. Magadh University ke NEP pattern me har paper 100 marks ka hota hai (CIA 30 + ESE 70) aur pass hone ke liye 45% marks chahiye hote hain, jo is website ki exam pattern guide me bataya gaya hai. Absent hone par us paper me zero marks milte hain, isliye woh paper pass nahi hota. Usi session me us paper ki dobara pariksha nahi hoti — university me fail paper ke liye koi alag supplementary exam nahi hota. Aapko us paper ko agle session me dobara dena hota hai, jab us semester ka exam form bhara jata hai — iski poori prakriya is website ki back paper guide me di gayi hai.

Kya Poora Saal Kharab Ho Jata Hai?

Nahi — ek ya do paper me absent hone se poora saal kharab nahi hota; sirf un papers ka nuksan hota hai, baaki papers ke marks barkarar rehte hain. Jo paper aapne de diye hain, unke marks aapke result me waise hi rahenge, aur agle session me aapko sirf wahi paper dobara dena hota hai jo chhoota tha. Isi liye ek paper chhootne par ghabrakar baaki papers chhodna sabse badi galti hoti hai — baaki papers achhe se dein, taaki dobara sirf chhoote hue paper ki taiyaari karni pade. Apne semester ke promotion aur back paper ke niyam apne college office se zaroor confirm kar lein, kyunki kuch courses me aage badhne ki shartein alag ho sakti hain.

Practical Ya Viva Me Absent Hone Par Kya Karein?

Practical ya viva me absent hone par turant apne college ke department ya office se baat karein — practical ka schedule aur marks college ke haath me hote hain, isliye aage ka rasta wahi batayega. Practical exam theory se pehle aur apne-apne college me hota hai — jaise UG 7th Semester (session 2023-27) ke practical aur viva 18 se 23 November 2026 ke beech ho rahe hain, aur theory exam 26 se 30 November 2026 ke beech hai. Practical ke marks college portal par upload karta hai aur unki hard copy 05/12/2026 tak university ko bhejni hai, jo is website ki 7th semester guide me bataya gaya hai. Agar aap practical ke din kisi genuine wajah se nahi pahunch paye, to bina deri kiye department se milen aur apni wajah likhit me batayen — jitni jaldi sampark karenge, utna behtar hoga. Kisi bhi vyakti ke "paise dekar practical pass karwa doonga" ke dawe par bharosa na karein — aisa karna galat hai aur pakde jaane par badi pareshani ho sakti hai.

Internal Exam (CIA) Me Absent Hone Par Kya Hota Hai?

Internal exam (CIA) me absent hone par aam taur par us test me zero marks milte hain, aur iska faisla aapke college aur department ke niyam se hota hai — isliye turant apne teacher ya HOD se baat karein. CIA ke 30 marks me mid-semester test, seminar/quiz/assignment aur attendance/conduct ke marks hote hain, aur university ke ek official exam notice (memo CEO/1104/2026, dated 06/11/2026 — UG 3rd Semester 2025-29) me admit card ke liye 75% attendance aur internal exam pass ki shart likhi thi. Kai colleges me bimari jaise genuine karan par internal test dobara dene ka mauka mil jata hai, lekin yeh college par nirbhar karta hai — isliye internal miss hote hi teacher ko suchit karna hi sabse sahi kadam hai. Internal ke marks exam pattern guide ke hisaab se ESE se alag hote hain, aur fail paper dobara dene par pehle wale CIA marks barkarar rehte hain.

Medical Emergency Ya Genuine Wajah Ho To Kya Karein?

Agar exam chhootne ki wajah bimari ya koi badi emergency hai, to uske saboot sambhal kar rakhein aur college ko turant likhit me suchit karein — bina bataye chup rehna sabse nuksan wala kaam hota hai. Doctor ka medical certificate, hospital ke kagaz ya emergency ka koi saboot apne paas rakhein, kyunki college ya university ko wajah batate samay yeh kagaz kaam aate hain. Yaad rakhein ki exam form bharte samay hi eligibility tay hoti hai — admit card milne ke baad bhi exam wale din present hona aapki zimmedari hoti hai, isliye exam se pehle apna centre, samay aur admit card pehle se check karke rakhein. Exam ke din ki poori checklist is website ki exam-ke-din guide me di gayi hai.

Absent Hone Ke Baad Aage Kya-Kya Karna Hai?

  1. Baaki papers zaroor dein — Ek paper chhootne par baaki papers chhodna galti hogi; baaki papers ke marks barkarar rahenge aur dobara sirf chhoota hua paper dena hoga.
  2. Result aane par apna status dekhein — Result student portal par dekhein; chhoote hue paper me fail ya back paper likha milega, to uski jaankari is website ki result guide se le sakte hain.
  3. Back paper ki prakriya samjhein — Agle session me us paper ke liye exam form bharna hota hai; fees aur form ki aakhri tareekh ki jaankari exam form guide aur college notice se lein, aur back paper guide ke steps follow karein.
  4. College office se confirm karein — Apne semester ke promotion, back paper aur agle exam form ke baare me college office se likhit ya notice ke hisaab se confirm karein; har college ki prakriya me thoda farak ho sakta hai.
  5. Agle exam ki taiyaari pehle se shuru karein — Chhoote hue paper ki taiyaari ke liye previous year question papers aur syllabus guide ka istemal karein, taaki dobara wahi paper na chhoote.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University exam me ek paper me absent ho gaye to kya hoga?
A: Us paper me aap fail mane jayenge, lekin poora semester kharab nahi hoga. Usi session me dobara exam nahi hota — agle session me us paper ko back paper ke roop me dobara dena hota hai, aur baaki papers ke marks barkarar rehte hain.

Q2. Absent hone par kya dobara exam de sakte hain?
A: Haan — agle session me us paper ke liye exam form bharna hota hai. Fail paper ke liye koi alag supplementary exam nahi hota, isliye back paper ki prakriya se hi dobara exam diya jata hai; CIA ke marks barkarar rehte hain aur sirf ESE dobara dena hota hai.

Q3. Practical exam chhoot gaya to kya karein?
A: Turant apne college ke department ya office se baat karein — practical ka schedule aur marks college ke haath me hote hain. Jaise 7th semester ke practical ke marks ki hard copy 05/12/2026 tak university ko bhejni hai, isliye deri na karein.

Q4. Internal exam miss ho gaya to kya hoga?
A: Aam taur par us internal test me zero marks milte hain, aur iska faisla college ke niyam se hota hai. Bimari jaise genuine karan par kuch colleges dobara mauka de dete hain — isliye turant teacher ya HOD se baat karein aur medical certificate jaise saboot rakhein.

Q5. Exam me absent hone ke baad saal bachane ke liye sabse pehle kya karein?
A: Sabse pehle baaki papers zaroor dein, phir result aane par apna status dekhein aur back paper guide ke hisaab se agle session ke exam form ki taiyaari karein. College office se apne semester ke niyam confirm karna na bhoolein.
