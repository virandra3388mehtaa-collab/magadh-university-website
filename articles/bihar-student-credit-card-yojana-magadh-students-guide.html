Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana (BSCC) — ₹4 Lakh Education Loan Kaise Lein

Magadh University me padhne wale kai students ke liye fees, hostel aur books ka kharcha badi pareshani hoti hai. Bihar sarkar ki Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana (BSCC) isi pareshani ke liye hai — iske tahat 12th pass students ko uchch shiksha ke liye ₹4 lakh tak ka education loan milta hai. Yojana Bihar sarkar ke Shiksha Vibhag ki hai aur Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta Yojana (7 Nischay Yuva Mission) ke tahat chalti hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki kaun aavedan kar sakta hai, kaun se documents chahiye, official portal par form kaise bharein aur DRCC verification ke baad loan kaise milta hai. Ye sab apne shabdon me likha gaya hai; loan ki rakam, byaaj aur shartein samay ke saath badal sakti hain — aakhri jaankari hamesha official portal se hi lein.

Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana kya hai

  • ₹4 lakh tak ka loan — yojana ke tahat 12th pass students ko uchch shiksha ke liye ₹4 lakh tak ka education loan milta hai.
  • Byaaj-mukt (2026 ke niyam ke anusaar) — 2026 ke niyam ke anusaar is loan par byaaj nahi lagta. Byaaj aur shartein sarkar ke aadesh se badal sakti hain, isliye aavedan se pehle official portal par current niyam zaroor check karein.
  • Kis kharche ke liye — loan ki rakam course ki fees, hostel kharcha, books, laptop aur padhai se jude doosre kharchon ke liye milti hai.
  • Kaun se course — sarkar se manyata-prapt sansthanon me chalane wale degree, professional, technical aur polytechnic course isme aate hain. Magadh University ke UG aur PG course bhi is daira me aate hain, jab tak sansthan yojana ke tahat approved ho.
  • Kab tak chalegi — Bihar sarkar ne is yojana ko vitt varsh 2030-31 tak badha diya hai, isliye aane wale saalon me bhi iska labh liya ja sakta hai.

Eligibility — kaun aavedan kar sakta hai

  • Bihar ka niwasi hona chahiye — aavedak ko Bihar ka sthayi niwasi hona chahiye. Niwas praman patra iska saboot hota hai.
  • 12th pass hona chahiye — aavedak ne Class 12 (Intermediate) ya uske barabar ki pariksha pass ki ho.
  • Umar 25 saal se zyada nahi — yojana ke niyam ke anusaar aavedak ki umar 25 saal se adhik nahi honi chahiye.
  • Approved sansthan me admission — aavedak ka kisi manyata-prapt course aur sansthan me admission hona chahiye, aur course poora karna bhi shart hai.
  • Magadh ke students ke liye — agar aap Magadh University ke kisi college ya department me UG/PG course me admission le chuke hain, to aap is yojana ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain. Admission ka tareeka is UG guide me bataya gaya hai.

Aavedan kaise karein — step by step

  1. Official portal kholein — 7 Nischay Yuva Upmission ki official website 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in kholein. Yahi yojana ka official portal hai — kisi aur site ya agent se form na bharein.
  2. New Applicant Registration karein — homepage par "New Applicant Registration" button par click karein. Apna naam, email ID, mobile number aur Aadhaar number likhein aur OTP se verification poora karein.
  3. Login karke Personal Information bharein — registration ke baad login karein aur Personal Information wala section poora bharein.
  4. BSCC scheme chunein — scheme chunne ke option me "Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC)" chunein aur Common Application Form (CAF) me apni personal, address, parivarik aay aur academic details sahi-sahi bharein.
  5. Form jama karein aur acknowledgement number rakhein — form submit karne ke baad ek acknowledgement number milta hai. Ise safe jagah likh kar rakhein — aage ki har jaankari ke liye yahi number kaam aayega.
  6. DRCC appointment ka intezaar karein — aapke jile ka District Registration and Counseling Center (DRCC) SMS aur email se appointment ki tareekh dega.
  7. DRCC par documents verification karwayein — appointment wale din self-attested documents ke saath DRCC jayein. Wahan documents ki jaanch hogi.
  8. Bank se loan sanction — documents verify hone aur bank se loan approve hone ke baad aapko bataya jayega ki Student Credit Card aur loan sanction letter kab milega. Card aur letter milne ke baad bank me jaa kar zaroori kagzi kaam poora karein — uske baad loan ki rakam di jayegi.

Aavedan ke liye zaroori documents

  • Bhara hua Common Application Form
  • Aadhaar card — student aur mata-pita / abhibhavak ka
  • PAN card — student aur mata-pita / abhibhavak ka
  • Class 10 aur Class 12 ki marksheet aur passing certificate
  • College / university se admission proof — admission letter
  • Sansthan se fee structure letter — course-wise aur saal-wise fees
  • Approved course structure (jahan manga jaye)
  • Parivarik aay praman patra (pichhle saal ka income certificate) — iska tareeka is guide me bataya gaya hai
  • Pichhle 6 mahine ka bank statement aur pichhle 2 saal ke Income Tax Return (agar parivar ITR bharta ho)
  • Niwas ka saboot — passport, voter ID, driving licence ya niwas praman patra
  • Passport size photo — student, mata-pita aur guarantor ki
  • Scholarship letter (agar koi scholarship mili ho)
  • Ye ek aam list hai — sahi list ke liye official portal aur DRCC se di gayi jaankari hi dekhein. Admission aur exam ke liye zaroori documents ki poori list is documents guide me bhi hai.
Zaroori baat: Aavedan me galat jaankari ya farzi documents dena yojana ke niyam ke khilaf hai — aisa karne par aavedan reject ho sakta hai aur aage kanooni pareshani bhi ho sakti hai. Saare documents asli aur sahi hone chahiye. Koi agent ya dalal aapse paise lekar form bharne ka dawa kare to usse bachein — official portal par aavedan khud, muft me hota hai.

Loan milne ke baad kya dhyaan rakhein

  • Sanction letter dhyaan se padhein — loan chukane (repayment) ki shartein, samay aur tareeka bank aur yojana ke niyam se tay hote hain aur sanction letter me likhe hote hain. Letter milte hi use dhyaan se padhein aur koi baat samajh na aaye to bank se poochhein.
  • Loan sirf padhai par kharch karein — loan ki rakam sirf fees, books, laptop, hostel aur padhai se jude kharchon ke liye hai. Ise kisi aur kaam me kharch na karein.
  • Course poora karein — yojana ki shart hai ki course poora kiya jaye. Beech me course chhodne par loan ki shartein badal sakti hain.
  • Saare kagaz sambhal kar rakhein — acknowledgement number, sanction letter, Student Credit Card aur bank ke kagaz ki copy apne paas zaroor rakhein.

Magadh students ke liye doosri madad

  • PMS Scholarship — Post Matric Scholarship ke baare me is scholarship guide me bataya gaya hai. Scholarship aur BSCC loan dono ka labh alag-alag niyam se milta hai — dono ki shartein alag se check karein.
  • Free study resources — SWAYAM aur NPTEL jaise muft resources se padhai ka kharcha kam kiya ja sakta hai — is guide me bataya gaya hai.
  • Documents taiyaar rakhein — admission aur exam ke liye kaun se documents chahiye, ye is list me dekhein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana me kitna loan milta hai?
A: Yojana ke tahat ₹4 lakh tak ka education loan milta hai. 2026 ke niyam ke anusaar is par byaaj nahi lagta — current niyam official portal par check karein.

Q2. Kaun aavedan kar sakta hai?
A: Bihar ka niwasi, 12th pass, umar 25 saal se zyada nahi, aur kisi manyata-prapt sansthan me admission hona chahiye.

Q3. Aavedan kahan karein?
A: Official portal 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in par "New Applicant Registration" se. Uske baad DRCC par documents verification hoti hai aur bank loan sanction karta hai.

Q4. Loan ki rakam kis kaam aati hai?
A: Course fees, hostel kharcha, books, laptop aur padhai se jude doosre kharchon ke liye.

Q5. Kya Magadh University ke students aavedan kar sakte hain?
A: Haan — agar aap Magadh University ke kisi approved course me admission le chuke hain, to aap is yojana ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain, bas eligibility aur documents poore hone chahiye.
