Magadh University Vocational Courses Admission Kaise Lein — BCA, BBM, MBA, MCA Samarth Portal Guide

BCA, BBM, MBA, MCA jaise courses Magadh University ke vocational / self-finance courses hain. Inme admission ka tareeka BA, B.Sc aur B.Com jaise aam UG courses se alag hai — inka aavedan magadhonline.in se nahi, Samarth Portal (magadhuniversityadm.samarth.edu.in) se hota hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki kaun se vocational courses hote hain, Samarth portal par aavedan kaise karein, merit list kahan check karein, kaun se documents chahiye aur khali seat par on-spot admission kaise hota hai. Ye sab apne shabdon me likha gaya hai; dates, fees aur merit list ki aakhri jaankari hamesha official notice se hi lein.

Vocational / self-finance course ka matlab kya hai

  • Job-oriented courses — BCA, BBM, MBA, MCA, B.Sc Biotech jaise courses khaas kaam ya peshe ki taiyaari ke liye banaye gaye hain. Inme padhai ke saath practical aur skill par zyada zor hota hai.
  • Self-finance matlab — ye courses university ke aam sarkari-anudanit courses se alag, self-finance mode me chalte hain. Inki fees aur admission prakriya aam UG courses se alag hoti hai — fees kitni hai, ye official admission notice me likha hota hai.
  • Admission Samarth Portal se — in courses ka aavedan, merit list aur Offer Letter sab Samarth Portal (magadhuniversityadm.samarth.edu.in) par hota hai. BA, B.Sc, B.Com ka admission magadhonline.in se hota hai — dono portal ko ek na samjhein. Aam UG admission ka tareeka is UG guide me bataya gaya hai.

Magadh University me kaun se vocational courses hote hain

University ke official notices ke anusaar in courses me admission hota hai. Course ki list har saal ke notice se badal sakti hai — aakhri list Samarth portal ke "Browse Programmes" page par dekhein.

  • Graduate (UG) level — BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications), BBM (Bachelor of Business Management), B.Sc. (Biotechnology), B.Sc. (IT), B.Sc. (Hons.) Environmental & Water Management, B.Sc. (Biochemistry), B.Lis. (Bachelor of Library Science), BASPSM, Bachelor of Physiotherapy, Bachelor in Hotel Management and Catering Technology (BHM&CT).
  • Postgraduate (PG) level — MBA, MCA, M.Sc. (Information Technology), M.Lis. (Master of Library Science).
  • Diploma / Certificate — PG Diploma in Yoga Science, Diploma in Foreign Languages (Spanish, German, French).
  • Kaun sa course kiske liye — BCA computer aur software ki taraf pehla kadam hai; BBM business aur management ki taraf; MBA aur MCA graduation ke baad ke professional course hain. Apni ruchi aur aage ki yojana dekh kar hi course chunein — sirf naam ya bheed dekh kar nahi.

Samarth Portal par aavedan kaise karein

  1. Official portal par jayein — magadhuniversityadm.samarth.edu.in kholein. Admission se judi jaankari magadhuniversity.ac.in par bhi dekhein.
  2. Register karein — pehli baar aavedan kar rahe hain to apna naam, mobile number aur email se naya account banayein. Application Number / User ID aur password safe jagah likh kar rakhein — merit list aur Offer Letter ke liye yahi login kaam aayega.
  3. Course aur college chunein — jis vocational course me admission chahiye aur jis department ya college me padhna hai, use dhyaan se chunein.
  4. Apni details bharein — form me apna naam, 12th ya graduation ke marks, board/university aur session sahi-sahi likhein. Details apni marksheet se milakar bharein.
  5. Documents upload karein — zaroori documents ki scan copy upload karein. Documents ki list neeche di gayi hai.
  6. Fees jama karein aur form jama karein — aavedan ki fees official tareeke se hi jama karein. Fees kitni hai, ye admission notice me likha hota hai — is article me koi fees nahi likhi gayi hai. Form jama karne ke baad uski print ya screenshot apne paas rakhein.

Merit list kaise check karein aur admission kaise pakka karein

  • Merit list round-wise aati hai — vocational courses ki merit list Samarth Portal par round-wise jaari hoti hai. Session 2026-27 ke liye pehli merit list September 2026 me, B.Lib ki doosri aur baaki courses ki teesri merit list 15 October 2026 ko jaari hui thi — aakhri sthiti Samarth portal par check karein.
  • Apna naam kaise check karein — Samarth Portal par apne Application Number / User ID aur password se login karein, phir merit status dekhein. Apna naam list me khoj kar check karein.
  • Offer Letter download karein — merit list me naam aane ke baad Samarth portal se apna Offer Letter download karein. Ye letter admission ke samay kaam aayega.
  • Department / college me report karein — Offer Letter aur original documents ke saath usme likhi last date tak allotted department ya college me report karein. Document verification aur fees jama karne ke baad hi admission pakka hota hai. Der ho jaye to seat kisi aur ko mil sakti hai.

Khali seat par on-spot admission (session 2026-27)

  • On-spot namankan — D.S.W. notice D.S.W/170/26 ke anusaar, session 2026-27 ke self-finance / vocational courses ki sweekrit aur khali seaton par on-spot namankan 23 November 2026 tak karne ka aadesh diya gaya hai.
  • Kaun le sakta hai — sirf wohi students jinhone M.U. Samarth portal par namankan ke liye pehle se aavedan kiya tha. Jisne Samarth par form hi nahi bhara, uske liye ye on-spot nahi hai.
  • Kaun se course nahi — Physiotherapy, B.Lis. aur M.Lis. is on-spot namankan me shamil nahi hain.
  • Namankan kaun lega — sambandhit college ke Principal / Prabhari Principal / Nideshak / Samanvayak on-spot namankan karenge. Kis college me kis course ki kitni seat khali hai, ye sirf college hi bata sakta hai — isliye turant apne college ya department se sampark karein.
Zaroori baat: On-spot admission ki aakhri tareekh 23 November 2026 hai — aaj 22 November hai, isliye der na karein. Tareekh aur shartein official notice D.S.W/170/26 ke anusaar likhi gayi hain; admission lene se pehle college ya department se ek baar zaroor confirm kar lein.

Admission ke liye zaroori documents

  • Samarth Portal se download kiya gaya Offer Letter
  • Online Application Form ki printout
  • 10th ki marksheet aur passing certificate
  • 12th (Intermediate) ki marksheet aur passing certificate
  • Graduation ki marksheet aur provisional / degree certificate (PG courses ke liye)
  • Passport size photo aur Photo ID — Aadhaar card ya koi sarkari ID
  • Caste certificate (agar aarakshan ka labh lena ho)
  • Ye ek aam list hai — sahi list ke liye admission notice aur Samarth portal par di gayi jaankari hi dekhein. Documents ki poori taiyaari ke liye documents guide bhi dekh sakte hain.

Eligibility — aam taur par kya chahiye

  • BCA aur doosre UG vocational courses — aam taur par 12th (Intermediate) pass hona chahiye. Har course ki sahi eligibility us saal ke official admission notice me likhi hoti hai — aavedan se pehle notice zaroor padhein.
  • MCA — aam taur par BCA degree 45% marks ke saath, ya B.Sc./B.A. jisme Computer Applications / Computer Science ek vishay raha ho aur Bachelor level par Mathematics pass ho. Sahi shartein official notice se hi lein.
  • M.Sc. IT — aam taur par B.Sc., BCA ya teen saal ki Bachelor degree — Electronics, Computer Science, Mathematics ya Physics vishay ke saath. Sahi shartein official notice se hi lein.
  • MBA — graduation ke baad ka course hai; sahi eligibility aur chunav ka tareeka official admission notice me dekhein.

Aavedan karte samay aam galtiyan

  • Galat portal par form bharna — vocational courses ka form Samarth Portal par bharta hai, magadhonline.in par nahi. Galat portal par form bharne se aavedan nahi mana jayega.
  • Merit list na check karna — kai students form bhar kar merit list check karna bhool jate hain. List round-wise aati hai, isliye Samarth portal par baar-baar check karein.
  • Offer Letter download na karna — naam aane ke baad Offer Letter download karna zaroori hai; bina iske college me verification nahi hogi.
  • Last date ka intezaar karna — aakhri din portal par bheed ya technical pareshani ho sakti hai; aavedan aur verification pehle hi kar lein.
  • Form ki copy na rakhna — jama kiye gaye form, Offer Letter aur fees ki raseed ki print ya screenshot apne paas zaroor rakhein. Admission ke baad registration card bhi milega — uska tareeka is guide me bataya gaya hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. BCA me admission ke liye kaun se portal par aavedan karein?
A: BCA ek vocational / self-finance course hai, isliye iska aavedan Samarth Portal (magadhuniversityadm.samarth.edu.in) par hota hai, magadhonline.in par nahi.

Q2. Vocational courses ki merit list kahan dekhein?
A: Merit list Samarth Portal par round-wise jaari hoti hai. Apne Application Number / User ID aur password se login karke merit status check karein.

Q3. Merit list me naam aane ke baad kya karna hai?
A: Samarth portal se Offer Letter download karein aur usme likhi last date tak original documents ke saath allotted department ya college me report karein. Verification aur fees ke baad hi admission pakka hota hai.

Q4. On-spot admission kis ke liye hai?
A: Session 2026-27 ke liye on-spot namankan 23 November 2026 tak sirf un students ke liye hai jinhone Samarth portal par pehle se aavedan kiya tha. Physiotherapy, B.Lis. aur M.Lis. isme shamil nahi hain.

Q5. Vocational courses ki fees kitni hai?
A: Fees har saal official admission notice se tay hoti hai, isliye is article me koi fees nahi likhi gayi hai. Aakhri jaankari admission notice aur Samarth portal se hi lein.
