Bihar Ki Dusri Scholarships — PMS Ke Alawa Magadh Students Ke Liye Yojanayein

Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) ke baare me alag se guide me bataya ja chuka hai, lekin Bihar ke students ke liye wahi ekmatra yojana nahi hai. Bihar Sarkar aur Bharat Sarkar ki kai aur yojanayein hain, jo ladkiyon ko padhai ke liye protsahan deti hain, merit wale students ko inaam deti hain aur higher education ke liye loan ki suvidha deti hain. Is guide me unhi yojanaon ki jaankari hai — kaun apply kar sakta hai, kya milta hai aur kahan apply karna hai. Yojana ki rakam aur niyam samay-samay par badalte rehte hain, isliye aavedan karne se pehle official portal par aakhri jaankari zaroor padhein.

1. Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana — ladkiyon ke liye sabse badi yojana

  • Kya hai — Bihar Sarkar ki Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (MKUY) ladkiyon ki padhai ko badhava dene ke liye hai. Isme janm se lekar graduation tak alag-alag padav par sahayata milti hai.
  • Kya milta hai — 2026 me report ki gayi rakam ke anusar, Intermediate (Class 12) pass karne par ₹25,000 aur graduation poora karne par ₹50,000 ek baar me Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) se Aadhaar se jude bank khate me milte hain. Ye rakam avivahit ladkiyon ke liye hai — graduation tak avivahit rehne par hi graduation wali rakam milti hai.
  • Kaun apply kar sakta hai — Bihar ki nivasi ladkiyan, jinhone Bihar ke kisi manyata-prapt board ya university se Intermediate ya graduation pass kiya ho.
  • Kahan apply karein — official MedhaSoft portal (medhasoft.bih.nic.in) par Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (Snatak) module se. Isi portal par application aur payment ki sthiti bhi dekhi ja sakti hai.

2. Mukhyamantri Medhavriti Yojana — SC/ST ladkiyon ke liye merit protsahan

  • Kya hai — Ye yojana Bihar ki Anusuchit Jati (SC) aur Anusuchit Janjati (ST) ki ladkiyon ke liye hai, jo Intermediate me achhe ank laati hain.
  • Kya milta hai — Intermediate me first division pass karne par ₹15,000 aur second division pass karne par ₹10,000 ek baar ki sahayata ke roop me milte hain.
  • Kaun apply kar sakta hai — Bihar ki sthayi nivasi SC/ST ladkiyan, jinhone kisi manyata-prapt sansthan se Class 12 first ya second division se pass kiya ho.
  • Kahan apply karein — MedhaSoft portal (medhasoft.bih.nic.in) par online aavedan hota hai.

3. Mukhyamantri Balak/Balika Protsahan Yojana — Matric pass students ke liye

  • Kya hai — Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) se Matric (Class 10) pass karne wale students ke liye protsahan yojana hai. Ye ladke aur ladkiyon, dono ke liye hai.
  • Kya milta hai — Matric me first division pass karne par ₹10,000 ek baar ki sahayata milti hai. SC/ST students ko second division pass karne par bhi ₹8,000 milte hain.
  • Kahan apply karein — MedhaSoft portal (medhasoft.bih.nic.in) par online aavedan hota hai. Ye sahayata sirf usi saal ke liye milti hai, jis saal exam pass kiya gaya ho.

4. Mukhyamantri Atyant Pichhda Varg Medhavriti Yojana — EBC students ke liye

  • Kya hai — Atyant Pichhda Varg (EBC / BC-I) ke students ke liye merit yojana hai, jo BSEB Matric exam me first division laate hain.
  • Kya milta hai — First division pass karne par ₹10,000 ek baar ki sahayata Aadhaar se jude bank khate me DBT se milti hai.
  • Kaun apply kar sakta hai — Bihar ke sthayi nivasi EBC (BC-I) varg ke students, jinhone BSEB se Matric first division se pass kiya ho.
  • Kahan apply karein — MedhaSoft portal (medhasoft.bihar.gov.in / medhasoft.bih.nic.in) par.

5. PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship — Bharat Sarkar ki merit scholarship

  • Kya hai — Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan (PM-USP) Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship, Shiksha Mantralaya, Bharat Sarkar ki yojana hai. Ye un students ke liye hai, jo Class 12 me apne board ke top 20% (80th percentile se upar) me aaye hon.
  • Kya milta hai — Graduation ke pehle teen saalon me ₹12,000 saalana aur postgraduation me ₹20,000 saalana. Professional course jaise B.Tech me chauthe saal ₹20,000 milte hain.
  • Kaun apply kar sakta hai — Class 12 me top 20% me aane wale students, jo kisi manyata-prapt sansthan me regular degree course me padh rahe hon (distance ya diploma course wale nahi), aur jinki parivar ki saalana aay ₹4.5 lakh se zyada na ho.
  • Renewal — Har saal renewal ke liye saalana exam me kam se kam 50% ank aur 75% attendance chahiye hoti hai.
  • Kahan apply karein — National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) par. Fresh aur renewal ki window har saal portal par khulti hai — aakhri tareekh portal par hi dekhein.

6. Bihar Student Credit Card — higher education ke liye 0% byaaj wala loan

  • Kya hai — Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) yojana ke tahat Class 12 pass students ko higher education ke liye ₹4 lakh tak ka education loan milta hai. September 2025 se ye loan sabhi students ke liye 0% byaaj par hai — matlab sirf liya hua paisa hi wapas karna hota hai.
  • Kya milta hai — Course fees, hostel, books, study material aur laptop jaise kharchon ke liye ₹4 lakh tak ka loan. Loan Bihar State Education Finance Corporation (BSEFC) deta hai.
  • Kaun apply kar sakta hai — Bihar ke sthayi nivasi, jinhone Class 12 ya uske barabar ki exam pass ki ho, aur kisi manyata-prapt sansthan me yojana ke tahat manya course me admission liya ho. Graduation ke liye aayu 25 saal se zyada nahi honi chahiye; postgraduate course ke liye seema 30 saal hai.
  • Kahan apply karein — MNSSBY portal (7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in) par online registration, aur apne jile ke District Registration cum Counselling Centre (DRCC) par. Yojana ko 2030-31 tak badha diya gaya hai.

Apply karne se pehle ki checklist

  1. Ek yojana, ek aavedan — Ek hi result ke liye ek se zyada naam se aavedan na karein. Jaise Class 12 wali ₹25,000 ki rakam Kanya Utthan Yojana me hi milti hai — use doosre naam se dobara na maangein.
  2. Aadhaar-seeded bank account — Zyada tar yojanaon ka paisa DBT se Aadhaar se jude khate me aata hai. Apna bank khata Aadhaar se joda hua aur sakriya rakhein.
  3. Documents taiyaar rakhein — Marksheet, Aadhaar card, domicile (niwas) praman patra, caste praman patra (jahan lagu ho), bank passbook aur passport-size photo aam taur par maange jate hain.
  4. Sirf official portal — Aavedan sirf upar diye gaye official portal par karein. Koi agent ya cyber cafe wala "scholarship dilane" ke naam par paise maange to turant savdhaan ho jayein — sarkari scholarship ke liye aavedan par koi shulk nahi lagta.
  5. Rakam aur tareekh portal par verify karein — Yojana ki rakam, eligibility aur aakhri tareekh samay-samay par badalti hai. Is article me di gayi rakam 2026 me report ki gayi hai — aavedan se pehle official portal par aakhri jaankari zaroor padhein.
Zaroori baat: Koi bhi yojana aapko scholarship ki guarantee nahi deti — paisa tabhi milta hai jab aap eligibility poori karte hon aur aavedan sahi tareeke se verify ho jaye. Agar koi message ya call aapko "scholarship approve ho gayi, fees bharo" kahe, to woh dhokha hai. Sarkari portal kabhi phone par paise nahi maangta.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Kya ek student ek se zyada scholarship le sakta hai?
A: Kai yojanaon ke niyam me likha hota hai ki ek saath do sarkari scholarship nahi mil sakti — jaise PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship me koi aur merit scholarship ya fee waiver nahi honi chahiye. Har yojana ke niyam alag hain, isliye aavedan se pehle us yojana ki shartein padhein. Ek hi result ke liye do yojanaon me aavedan karne se aavedan reject bhi ho sakta hai.

Q2. Kanya Utthan Yojana ki ₹50,000 wali rakam kis ko milti hai?
A: Ye rakam un ladkiyon ko milti hai, jinhone Bihar ke kisi manyata-prapt university se graduation poora kiya ho aur graduation tak avivahit rahi hon. Aavedan MedhaSoft portal par hota hai aur paisa DBT se khate me aata hai.

Q3. Bihar Student Credit Card loan hai ya scholarship?
A: Ye loan hai, scholarship nahi. ₹4 lakh tak ka loan 0% byaaj par milta hai, lekin liya hua paisa course ke baad wapas karna hota hai. Isliye loan lene se pehle repayment ke niyam portal aur DRCC se samajh lein.

Q4. Scholarship ka paisa kab tak milta hai?
A: Iska koi ek nishchit samay nahi hai. Aavedan, college/institute se verification aur vibhag ki swikriti ke baad paisa DBT se khate me aata hai. Apne aavedan ki sthiti official portal par hi check karein aur apne college se bhi poochte rahein.
