Magadh University Scrutiny Aur Revaluation Kaise Karwayein — Re-checking Guide

Result dekhne ke baad kai students ko lagta hai ki kisi paper me unke number ummeed se kam aaye hain — ya to koi sawaal check hi nahi hua, ya jod me galti hui, ya evaluation me kami reh gayi. Aisi sthiti me university ke paas do alag tareeke hain: scrutiny aur revaluation. Dono ek jaise nahi hain, inki fees aur prakriya bhi alag hoti hai, aur dono ke liye aavedan ki ek nishchit khidki (window) hoti hai jo result ke baad khulti hai. Is guide me dono ka farq, aavedan ke steps, zaroori documents aur revised result ki jaankari apne shabdon me samjhayi gayi hai. Is article me koi fees ya last date nahi likhi gayi hai — ye har session ke examination notice se tay hoti hai aur badalti rehti hai, isliye sahi rakam aur aakhri tareekh hamesha official notice ya apne college se hi dekhein.

Scrutiny ke liye kaise apply karein

University scrutiny ki khidki examination notice ke zariye ghoshit karti hai — notice official website magadhuniversity.ac.in par aata hai. Pichhli prakriya ke anusaar steps is prakar hain:

  1. Examination notice dekhein: Result ke baad magadhuniversity.ac.in par examination notice dekhein — usme kaun se semester ke liye scrutiny khuli hai aur aakhri tareekh kya hai, ye likha hota hai.
  2. College ke exam section se form lein: Scrutiny ka form aam taur par apne college ke exam section se milta hai. Form par woh paper aur subject likhein jin par shaq hai.
  3. Notice me likhi per-paper fees jama karein: Fees har paper ke hisab se lagti hai aur rakam notice me likhi hoti hai. Payment ka tareeka (challan ya online) bhi notice ya college se pata karein — kisi bhi vyakti ko nakad paise dekar form bharwane se bachein.
  4. Form ke saath online marksheet ki copy lagayein: Apni online marksheet ki print copy form ke saath jodein. Admit card ki copy bhi saath rakhna behtar rehta hai.
  5. Form college me jama karein: Bhara hua form college ke exam section me jama karein aur jama karne ki raseed ya proof apne paas rakhein.
  6. Revised result ka intezaar karein: Scrutiny ke baad sanshodhit result wahi session portal par dikhta hai jahan aapne result dekha tha. Agar number badle to nayi marksheet usi hisab se banti hai.

Revaluation ke liye kaise apply karein

  1. Notice ka intezaar karein: Revaluation ke liye bhi university result ke baad ek notice nikalti hai — usme kaun se subjects ke theory papers ke liye aavedan ho sakta hai, ye likha hota hai.
  2. Form lein: Aavedan ka form official website magadhuniversity.ac.in par ya apne college se milta hai.
  3. Form bharein aur fees jama karein: Form me apna naam, registration/roll number, subject aur paper ki jaankari bharein. Per-paper fees notice ke anusaar jama karein — fees ki rakam aur payment ka tareeka har session me badal sakta hai, isliye notice hi antim hai.
  4. Form jama karein: Form college ke zariye ya notice me likhe tareeke se jama karein. Jama karne ki raseed apne paas rakhein.
  5. Result ka intezaar karein: Revaluation ke baad sanshodhit result official website aur result portal par ghoshit hota hai. Revaluation ke baad milne wale number antim mane jate hain — chahe badhein ya na badhein.

Dhyan dein: Revaluation ka aavedan sirf theory papers ke liye hota hai. Practical ya internal (CIA) ke number par aam taur par revaluation nahi hota — aisi koi samasya ho to pehle college ke exam section se poochhein.

Zaroori documents — aavedan se pehle taiyaar rakhein

  • Online marksheet ki print copy — jisme woh paper aur number dikh rahe hon jin par aavedan karna hai.
  • Admit card ki copy — pehchan aur paper ki pushti ke liye.
  • Registration number / roll number — form par sahi likhna zaroori hai.
  • Fees ki raseed ya challan — payment ka proof.
  • College se mila koi letter ya forward — agar college ke zariye jama kar rahe hain to college ki taraf se bhi form aage bheja jata hai.

Fees aur last date kaise pata karein

Scrutiny aur revaluation ki fees aur aakhri tareekh har session ke examination notice se tay hoti hai. Kisi bhi purani fees ko aaj ki fees mat maniye — sahi rakam aur date ke liye ye teen jagah dekhein:

  • magadhuniversity.ac.in — Examination / Notice Board section me scrutiny-revaluation ka notice.
  • Apne college ka exam section / notice board — college ke paas notice aur form dono milte hain.
  • Result portal (magadhonline.in) — revised result yahin dikhta hai.

Notice nikalne ke baad aavedan ki khidki aam taur par kuch hi hafton ki hoti hai, isliye result dekhte hi notice par nazar rakhna shuru kar dein — der hone par aavedan nahi liya jata.

Result me "Pending" dikhe to kya karein

Agar aapke online scorecard par kisi paper ke saamne Pending likha ho — jaise practical ya CIA ke number abhi nahi aaye hon — to ye scrutiny ya revaluation ka mamla nahi hai. Aisi sthiti me apne admit card aur pichli marksheet ki copy ke saath college ke principal ke zariye Bodh Gaya Examination Section ko aavedan dena hota hai, aur ye bhi result ke kuch hi dinon ke andar karna hota hai. Apne college se turant poochhein ki aapke mamle me kya karna hai.

Zaroori baat: Scrutiny ya revaluation ke naam par koi bhi vyakti agar paise mange, aapki login details mange ya "number badhwa dunga" kahe, to woh farzi hai. Number sirf official prakriya se badalte hain — kisi agent, WhatsApp forward ya "setting" ke chakkar me na padein aur apni user ID-password kisi se share na karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Scrutiny aur revaluation me kaun sa behtar hai?
A: Dono alag kaam karte hain. Jod ki galti ya bina check hue sawaal ke liye scrutiny, aur dobara mulyankan chahne par revaluation. Pehle apni marksheet dhyaan se dekhein, phir tay karein — college ke exam section se salah lena bhi behtar rehta hai.

Q2. Scrutiny/revaluation ki fees kitni hai?
A: Fees per-paper hoti hai aur har session ke examination notice se tay hoti hai. Sahi rakam notice ya college se hi dekhein — purani fees ko aaj ki fees mat maniye.

Q3. Aavedan ki last date kab hoti hai?
A: Aavedan ki khidki result ke baad khulti hai aur notice me likhi aakhri tareekh tak hi khuli rehti hai. Date nikalne ke baad aavedan nahi liya jata, isliye result ke turant baad notice par nazar rakhein.

Q4. Scrutiny/revaluation ke baad number kam bhi ho sakte hain?
A: Scrutiny me sirf galti sudharti hai, isliye number badh bhi sakte hain aur wahi bhi reh sakte hain. Revaluation me dobara mulyankan hota hai aur uske baad milne wale number antim mane jate hain — isliye aavedan se pehle soch-samajh kar tay karein.

Q5. Revised result kahan dikhega?
A: Sanshodhit result wahi session portal (magadhonline.in) aur official website par dikhta hai jahan aapne pehle result dekha tha. Nayi marksheet bhi usi hisab se banti hai.

Q6. Kya practical ya internal ke number par bhi revaluation hota hai?
A: Aam taur par revaluation sirf theory papers ke liye hota hai. Practical ya CIA ke number me samasya ho to pehle apne college ke exam section se poochhein.
