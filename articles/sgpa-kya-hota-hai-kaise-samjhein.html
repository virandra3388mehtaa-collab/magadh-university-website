SGPA Kya Hota Hai Aur Kaise Samjhein — Seedha Guide

Marksheet dekhte waqt sabse zyada poochha jaane wala shabd hai SGPA. Iska poora naam hai Semester Grade Point Average — matlab ek semester me aapke sabhi subjects ke pradarshan ka ek ausat (average), jo grade points aur credits ke hisab se nikalta hai. SGPA sirf ek number nahi hai; yah batata hai ki us semester me aapka overall pradarshan kaisa raha.

SGPA kaise nikalta hai — seedha formula

Har subject ke do cheezein hoti hain: credit (us subject ka bhaar) aur grade point (us subject me mile grade ke hisab se ank). SGPA nikalne ka tareeka yah hai:

SGPA = (Sabhi subjects ke credit × grade point ka jod) ÷ (Sabhi subjects ke credits ka jod)

Ek chhota udaharan se samjhein (yah sirf samjhane ke liye udaharan hai, kisi khaas marksheet ka ank nahi):

SGPA, CGPA aur percentage me farq

  • SGPA: Sirf ek semester ka average.
  • CGPA: Poore course (sabhi semester) ka kul average — Cumulative Grade Point Average.
  • Percentage: Marks ko 100 me se batane ka tareeka. Percentage nikalne ka niyam har university ka alag ho sakta hai, isliye apni marksheet ya university ke niyam se hi percentage nikalein, kisi aur ke formula se nahi.

SGPA samajhne me aam galtiyan

  • Sirf marks jodna: SGPA marks ka seedha average nahi hai — isme credit ka bhaar judta hai, isliye zyada credit wale subject ka asar zyada hota hai.
  • Grade ko marks samajhna: Grade aur grade point alag cheezein hain. Grade point wahi lein jo aapki marksheet par diya gaya hai.
  • Dusre ki marksheet se milana: Har course aur session ke credit alag ho sakte hain, isliye SGPA ki tulna hamesha apne hi course ke hisab se karein.
  • Online calculator par poora bharosa: Calculator sirf hisab lagata hai. Aakhri aur sahi SGPA wahi hai jo aapki official marksheet par likha hai.
Yaad rakhein: Agar aapke hisab se nikla SGPA aur marksheet par likha SGPA alag ho, to marksheet wala hi sahi mana jayega. Fark dikhe to apne college se poochhein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. SGPA ka full form kya hai?
A: Semester Grade Point Average — ek semester ke sabhi subjects ka credit-weighted average.

Q2. SGPA kaise nikalein?
A: Har subject ke credit ko uske grade point se guna karein, sabka jod karein, aur kul credits se bhaag dein. Apni marksheet ke asli ank hi istemal karein.

Q3. SGPA aur CGPA me kya farq hai?
A: SGPA ek semester ka hota hai, CGPA poore course ke sabhi semester ka kul average hota hai.

Q4. Kya SGPA se percentage nikal sakte hain?
A: Percentage nikalne ka niyam university par nirbhar karta hai. Bina official niyam jaane koi bhi formula lagana galat ho sakta hai — apni marksheet ya college se niyam poochhein.
