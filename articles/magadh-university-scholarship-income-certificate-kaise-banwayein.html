Scholarship Ke Liye Income Certificate Kaise Banwayein — Magadh Students Guide

PMS scholarship aur Bihar ki kai doosri scholarship ke liye aavedan karte samay sabse pehle maanga jane wala kagaz Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra) hota hai. Isi se tay hota hai ki aapke parivar ki saalana aay scholarship ki shart ke andar aati hai ya nahi. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki income certificate kya hai, kaun banwa sakta hai, yeh kahan se banta hai, kaun se documents lagte hain aur Bihar me RTPS se online apply karne ke steps kya hain. Fees ya samay ki koi rakam is article me nahi di gayi hai, kyunki woh portal aur anchal karyalaya se tay hoti hai.

Income Certificate Kya Hai Aur Scholarship Me Kyun Chahiye?

Income Certificate sarkar dwara jaari woh kagaz hai, jo batata hai ki aapke parivar ki saalana aay kitni hai. Scholarship wali yojanaon me iska kaam yeh saabit karna hota hai ki student aarthik roop se us shreni me aata hai, jiske liye scholarship bani hai. PMS scholarship jaise aavedano me aay ki seema tay hoti hai — isliye scholarship ka form bharte samay income certificate ki copy lagani padti hai. Bina sahi income certificate ke scholarship ka aavedan adhura mana jata hai aur reject ho sakta hai.

Income Certificate Kaun Banwa Sakta Hai?

Bihar ka koi bhi niwasi apne parivar ki aay ka praman patra banwa sakta hai — iske liye koi khaas yogyata ya exam nahi hota. Scholarship ke liye aavedan karne wale student ke liye yeh certificate uske mata-pita ya abhibhavak ki aay par banta hai. Aavedan karte samay aapko apna naam, pata, parivar ke sadasyon ki jaankari aur aay ka srot batana hota hai. Jo jaankari aavedan me di jati hai, wahi certificate par chhapti hai — isliye galat aay likhne se bachein, kyunki jaanch me galat jaankari pakdi jane par certificate radd ho sakta hai.

Income Certificate Kahan Se Banta Hai?

Bihar me income certificate aapke anchal (block) karyalaya se banta hai, aur iske liye online aavedan Bihar sarkar ke RTPS portal (serviceonline.bihar.gov.in) par hota hai. Aap khud portal par aavedan kar sakte hain, ya apne najdeeki Common Service Centre (CSC) ya Vasudha Kendra se karwa sakte hain. Aavedan ke baad jaanch anchal karyalaya karta hai aur certificate online hi jaari hota hai, jise aap download karke print kar sakte hain. Kisi bhi vyakti ko "turant certificate banwa dene" ke naam par paise na dein — yeh kaam sirf official tareeke se hi hota hai.

Income Certificate Ke Liye Kaun Se Documents Chahiye?

Aadhaar card, niwas ka saboot (jaise ration card ya voter ID), passport size photo aur aay ka saboot (jaise pita/mata ki salary slip, ya kheti/mazdoori se aay ka swaghoshna patra) aam taur par maange jate hain. Aavedan ke liye aapka mobile number bhi chahiye hota hai, kyunki usi par aavedan ki sthiti ki jaankari aati hai. Documents ki aakhri list RTPS portal par aavedan karte samay dikhti hai — form bharne se pehle use padh lein aur saare documents ki scanned copy taiyaar rakhein.

Income Certificate Online Kaise Apply Karein? Step-by-Step

  1. RTPS portal kholein — Bihar sarkar ka RTPS portal serviceonline.bihar.gov.in kholein. Wahan "Apply Online" ya "Online Apply" ka option chunein.
  2. Seva chunein — Sevaon ki list me se "Income Certificate" (Aay Praman Patra) wali seva chunein. Form khulne par apna jila, anchal aur panchayat/ward saaf-saf chunein.
  3. Form bharein — Form me apna naam, pita/mata ka naam, pata, parivar ki saalana aay aur aay ka srot sahi-sahi bharein. Naam aur pata Aadhaar card jaisa hi likhein, taaki baad me koi galti na ho.
  4. Documents upload karein — Maange gaye documents ki scanned copy upload karein. Har document saaf dikhna chahiye — dhundli copy se aavedan atak sakta hai.
  5. Aavedan jama karein aur receipt lein — Form jama karne par aapko ek acknowledgement number ya receipt milegi. Use sambhal kar rakhein — isi se aap apne aavedan ki sthiti check kar sakte hain.
  6. Certificate download karein — Jaanch poori hone par certificate portal par taiyaar ho jata hai. Apne acknowledgement number se sthiti check karein aur certificate download karke print kar lein.

Income Certificate Banne Me Kitna Samay Lagta Hai?

Iska koi ek nishchit samay is article me nahi likha ja sakta, kyunki samay jaanch aur anchal karyalaya ke kaam par nirbhar karta hai — lekin RTPS ki receipt par aapke aavedan ki tay tareekh (due date) likhi hoti hai, wahi aakhri jaankari hai. Aam taur par certificate kuch din me taiyaar ho jata hai, lekin kabhi-kabhi jaanch ke liye zyada samay lag sakta hai. Isliye scholarship ki last date ka intezar na karein — certificate ke liye aavedan scholarship ka form bharne se kai din pehle hi kar dein, taaki der hone par bhi samay bacha rahe.

Income Certificate Ki Fees Kitni Hai?

Fees ki koi rakam is article me nahi di gayi hai, kyunki shulk portal aur seva kendra ke hisaab se alag ho sakta hai — sahi rakam aavedan karte samay RTPS portal par hi dikhti hai. Aam taur par iska shulk mamuli hota hai. Fees dein to hamesha official receipt lein. Koi bhi vyakti ya dukaan agar certificate ke naam par zyada paise maange, to pehle portal par sahi shulk check karein.

Scholarship Form Me Income Certificate Kaise Lagayein?

Scholarship ka form bharte samay income certificate ki scanned copy upload karni hoti hai — certificate par likhi aay aur form me bhari gayi aay ek hi honi chahiye. Certificate purana nahi hona chahiye: kai scholarship me chhah mahine se zyada purana certificate nahi mana jata, isliye aavedan se pehle yojana ki shartein padhein. Agar certificate par naam, pata ya aay me koi galti ho, to scholarship ka form bharne se pehle use sudharwa lein — galat certificate se aavedan reject ho sakta hai.

Direct Official Link

  • Bihar RTPS portal (income certificate online apply): serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
  • Magadh University ki official website: magadhuniversity.ac.in — scholarship aur notice ki jaankari ke liye.
  • Student portal (result, admit card, exam form): magadhonline.in
Zaroori baat: Scholarship ka form bharne se pehle income certificate taiyaar rakhna chahiye. Aakhri din par certificate ke liye bhaagna scholarship chhootne ki sabse badi wajah hai — certificate ka aavedan aaj hi kar dein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Scholarship ke liye income certificate kaise banwayein?
A: Bihar ke RTPS portal serviceonline.bihar.gov.in par online aavedan karein — form bharein, documents upload karein, receipt sambhal kar rakhein aur jaanch poori hone par certificate download kar lein. Aap CSC ya Vasudha Kendra se bhi aavedan karwa sakte hain.

Q2. Income certificate ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
A: Aam taur par Aadhaar card, niwas ka saboot (ration card ya voter ID), passport size photo aur aay ka saboot (salary slip ya swaghoshna patra) maange jate hain. Aakhri list RTPS portal par form bharte samay dikhti hai.

Q3. Income certificate banne me kitna samay lagta hai?
A: Samay jaanch aur anchal karyalaya par nirbhar karta hai. RTPS ki receipt par aapke aavedan ki tay tareekh likhi hoti hai — wahi sahi jaankari hai. Scholarship ki last date se kai din pehle aavedan kar dein.

Q4. Kya purana income certificate scholarship me chalega?
A: Kai scholarship me chhah mahine se zyada purana certificate nahi mana jata. Aavedan se pehle yojana ki shartein padhein aur zaroorat ho to naya certificate banwa lein.
