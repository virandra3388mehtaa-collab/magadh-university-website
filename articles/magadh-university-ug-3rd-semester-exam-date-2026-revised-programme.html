Magadh University UG 3rd Semester Exam Date 2026 — Revised Programme (26 Nov – 2 Dec), Session 2025-29

Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ne UG 3rd Semester (B.A. / B.Sc. / B.Com., 4-year CBCS course, Session 2025-29) ka Revised Examination Programme jaari kiya hai — memo CEO/1106/2026, dinank 17 November 2026. Theory pariksha 26 November se 2 December 2026 tak hogi. Pehle wala karyakram (memo CEO/972, 31 August 2026) 18–25 November ka tha, jo ab laagu nahi hai. Neeche nayi date sheet, dono paali ka samay aur admit card ki shart di gayi hai.

Revised date sheet — poori list

  • 26 November 2026 (Thursday) — Major Course (MJC-3)
  • 27 November 2026 (Friday) — Major Course (MJC-4)
  • 28 November 2026 (Saturday) — Minor Course (MIC-3)
  • 29 November 2026 (Sunday) — Pariksha nahi hai
  • 30 November 2026 (Monday) — Multidisciplinary Course (MDC-3)
  • 1 December 2026 (Tuesday) — Ability Enhancement Course (AEC-3)
  • 2 December 2026 (Wednesday) — Skill Enhancement Course (SEC-3)

Har din dono paali me ek hi paper hota hai — apne subject ki sahi tareekh admit card par milayein.

Dono paali ka samay

  • Pehli paali — subah 10:00 se dopahar 1:00 baje tak: Arts (sabhi Social Science aur Humanities subjects)
  • Doosri paali — dopahar 2:00 se shaam 5:00 baje tak: sabhi Science aur Commerce subjects

Kendra par samay se pehle pahunchein, admit card aur photo ID saath rakhein.

Practical aur viva-voce ke ank kab tak

Revised programme ke note ke anusaar practical aur viva-voce ke ank 10 December 2026 tak portal par upload kiye jayenge. Ank na dikhen to apne college ke exam section se poochhein.

Admit card aur 75% attendance ki shart

Memo CEO/1104/2026 (6 November 2026) ke anusaar pariksha me baithne ke liye 75% varg upasthiti (class attendance) aur internal pariksha me pass hona zaroori hai. Admit card ke baare me poori jaankari — kab milega, kaise download karein, aur card par kya-kya check karein — hamare alag article me di gayi hai: Magadh University UG 3rd Semester Admit Card 2026 — Download Kaise Karein. Exam form aur shulk jama karne ki aakhri tareekh (chhoote hue chhatron ke liye, memo CEO/1107/2026) 21 November 2026 thi, jo ab beet chuki hai.

Zaroori baat: Sirf university ke official notice ko hi sahi maanein. Date sheet ya centre se judi koi bhi khabar social media par dikhe to use magadhuniversity.ac.in ya magadhonline.in par check karke hi yakeen karein.

Aane wali parikshayein — ek nazar me

31 August 2026 ke mool karyakram (memos CEO/969, CEO/970, CEO/971) ke anusaar aane wali parikshayein:

  • UG 7th Semester (Session 2023-27): theory 26 se 30 November 2026 (memo CEO/971)
  • PG 3rd Semester (Session 2025-27): theory 1 se 8 December 2026 (memo CEO/970)
  • UG 5th Semester (Session 2024-28): theory 10 se 15 December 2026 (memo CEO/969)

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University UG 3rd semester ki pariksha kab se hai?
A: Revised programme (CEO/1106/2026, 17 November 2026) ke anusaar theory pariksha 26 November se 2 December 2026 tak hai.

Q2. Pehle wala 18–25 November ka karyakram ab laagu hai?
A: Nahi. Purana karyakram (CEO/972, 31 August 2026) revised programme se badal diya gaya hai. Ab 26 November se 2 December wala karyakram hi maanya hai.

Q3. Arts aur Science ki pariksha ka samay alag kyun hai?
A: Arts (Social Science aur Humanities) ki pariksha subah 10:00–1:00 baje aur Science aur Commerce ki dopahar 2:00–5:00 baje hoti hai.

Q4. Practical ke ank kab tak aayenge?
A: Practical aur viva-voce ke ank 10 December 2026 tak portal par upload karne ka nirdesh hai.
