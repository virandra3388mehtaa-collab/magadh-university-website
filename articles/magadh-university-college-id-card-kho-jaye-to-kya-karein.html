Magadh University College ID Card Kho Jaye To Kya Karein — Duplicate ID Card Kaise Banwayein?

College ID card kho jana ek aam pareshani hai — aur exam ke dinon me to ye aur bhi chinta badha deta hai, kyunki exam centre par pehchan ke liye ID card manga ja sakta hai. Seedha jawab hai ki ID card kho jaye to sabse pehle apne college office me turant batayein aur duplicate ID card ke liye aavedan dein — duplicate card college se hi banta hai, uski fees aur samay har college me alag hota hai, isliye is article me koi rakam ya samay ka dawa nahi kiya gaya hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki card khone par pehle kya karein, duplicate ke liye kaun se documents chahiye, aavedan kaise karein aur exam ke din card na ho to kya karein.

ID Card Kho Jaye To Sabse Pehle Kya Karein?

Card khone par sabse pehle wahi jagah dobara dhyaan se dhoondhein, jahan aap aakhri baar gaye the — class, library, canteen ya bus — aur phir turant apne college office ko batayein. College office ko batane se do fayde hote hain — ek to aapka card kisi ko mile to woh college me jama kar dega, aur doosra, duplicate card ki prakriya turant shuru ho jayegi. Agar card ke saath koi aur zaroori cheez bhi kho gayi ho, to use bhi office ko batayein. Card khone ki khabar der se dene se exam ke din pareshani ho sakti hai, isliye is kaam ko kal par mat talen.

Duplicate ID Card Ke Liye Kaun Se Documents Chahiye?

Duplicate ID card ke liye aam taur par ye cheezein chahiye hoti hain — ek likhit aavedan (application), apni ek passport-size photo, aur apni pehchan ka koi saboot jaise admission receipt ya registration card. Kuch college card khone ki likhit jaankari bhi mangte hain. Sahi list har college me thodi alag ho sakti hai — isliye aavedan dene se pehle apne college office se ek baar zaroor pooch lein ki unhe kya-kya chahiye. Saare kagaz ek saath lekar jayenge to kaam ek hi baar me ho jayega aur baar-baar office ke chakkar nahi lagenge.

Duplicate ID Card Kaise Banwayein — Step-by-Step

Duplicate ID card banwane ke aam steps ye hain — pehle college office me card khone ki likhit jaankari dein, phir duplicate card ke liye aavedan form bharein, zaroori documents aur photo jama karein, aur college dwara batayi gayi fees jama karein.

  1. College office me jakar card khone ki baat batayein aur likhit aavedan dein.
  2. Duplicate ID card ka form lein aur use sahi jaankari ke saath bharein — naam, course, session aur roll number dhyaan se likhein.
  3. Passport-size photo aur pehchan ke kagaz (admission receipt ya registration card) saath me jama karein.
  4. College dwara batayi gayi fees jama karein aur uski raseed sambhal kar rakhein — raseed hi aapka saboot hai.
  5. College se poochhein ki duplicate card kab milega, aur us din card lene jayein — card milte hi naam, photo aur details dhyaan se check karein.

Duplicate ID Card Banne Me Kitna Samay Lagta Hai?

Duplicate ID card banne ka samay har college me alag hota hai — kuch college me ye ek-do din me ban jata hai, to kuch me kuch din lag sakte hain; iska koi ek nishchit samay is article me nahi likha gaya hai. Isliye card khote hi turant aavedan dena sabse zaroori hai, khaas taur par tab, jab exam najdeek ho. Agar exam bahut najdeek ho, to college office ko ye baat saaf batayein — kai college exam se pehle card jaldi banane me madad karte hain. Card milne tak apni fees ki raseed aur aavedan ki copy apne paas rakhein, taaki zaroorat padne par dikha sakein.

Duplicate ID Card Ki Fees Kitni Hoti Hai?

Duplicate ID card ki fees har college me alag hoti hai — iski koi ek rakam is article me nahi likhi gayi hai, kyunki fees college dwara tay ki jati hai. Fees kitni hai, ye college office se hi pata chalega. Fees jama karte samay raseed zaroor lein aur use sambhal kar rakhein. Dhyaan rakhein — ID card banane ke naam par college ke bahar koi vyakti aapse paise mange to use paise na dein; duplicate card sirf college office se hi banta hai.

Exam Ke Din ID Card Na Ho To Kya Karein?

Exam ke din ID card na ho, to ghabrayein nahi — admit card aur koi dusra pehchan patra (jaise Aadhaar card) saath lekar jayein aur exam se pehle apne college ya exam centre ke adhikari ko poori baat batayein. Exam centre par pehchan ke liye aam taur par admit card dekha jata hai, lekin kuch centre par college ID card bhi manga ja sakta hai — isliye exam se pehle apne college se pooch lein ki aapke mamle me kya chalega. Agar duplicate card ke liye aavedan de chuke hain, to uski raseed aur aavedan ki copy saath lekar jayein — ye dikhata hai ki aapne samay par kaam kiya hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. College ID card kho jaye to sabse pehle kya karein?
A: Sabse pehle wahi jagah dobara dhoondhein, jahan aap aakhri baar gaye the, aur phir turant apne college office ko batayein. Der karne se exam ke din pareshani ho sakti hai.

Q2. Duplicate ID card kaise banwayein?
A: College office me likhit aavedan dein, duplicate card ka form bharein, passport-size photo aur pehchan ke kagaz jama karein aur college dwara batayi gayi fees jama karein. Duplicate card sirf college office se hi banta hai.

Q3. Duplicate ID card banne me kitna samay lagta hai?
A: Samay har college me alag hota hai — kuch me ek-do din, kuch me kuch din lag sakte hain. Exam najdeek ho to college office ko ye baat saaf batayein aur aavedan turant dein.

Q4. Exam ke din ID card na ho to kya karein?
A: Admit card aur koi dusra pehchan patra (jaise Aadhaar card) saath lekar jayein, exam se pehle college ya centre ke adhikari ko batayein, aur duplicate card ke aavedan ki raseed saath rakhein.
