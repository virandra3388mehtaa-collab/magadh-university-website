Magadh University Degree Verification Kaise Karwayein

Degree milne ke baad kai baar uski jaanch (verification) karwani padti hai — naukri ke liye aavedan karte samay, kisi doosri university me admission lete samay ya bahar padhne ke liye. Verification ka matlab hai ki jis sansthan ko aapki degree dikhayi ja rahi hai, woh university se confirm karta hai ki degree asli hai aur us par likhi jaankari sahi hai. Kai students ko ye samajh nahi aata ki verification kaun karwata hai, kahan aavedan dena hai aur kaun se kagaz lagte hain. Is guide me poori prakriya samjhaya gaya hai. Shulk aur samay ki jaankari university ke niyam ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hai, isliye aakhri jaankari university office ya apne college se hi confirm karein.

1. Degree Verification kya hoti hai aur kab chahiye hoti hai

  • Kya hai — Degree verification me university apne record se milakar confirm karti hai ki degree asli hai, kis student ko, kis course aur kis session me jaari ki gayi thi.
  • Kab chahiye — Sarkari ya private naukri ke liye document verification ke samay, kisi doosri university me PG ya research admission ke samay, aur kabhi-kabhi bahar desh me padhai ya naukri ke liye aavedan karte samay verification maangi jati hai.
  • Kaun karwata hai — Aam taur par woh sansthan verification karwata hai, jahan aapne degree jama ki hai (jaise employer ya naya college). Kuch mamlo me student ko khud university se verified copy ya verification letter lena padta hai.
  • Verification aur attestation me farq — Verification ka matlab hai record se jaanch, jabki attestation ka matlab hai kagaz par muhar/hastakshar karke uski pramanikta batana. Dono alag kaam hain; aavedan wali jagah se pehle pooch lein ki unhe kya chahiye.

2. Verification ke liye kaun se documents lagte hain

  • Degree ya Provisional Certificate — Original degree ki copy; agar original degree abhi nahi mili hai to provisional certificate kaam aata hai.
  • Marksheet — Aakhri semester ya aakhri saal ki marksheet ki copy, jisse course aur session ki pushti ho.
  • Pehchan patra — Aadhaar Card ya koi aur sarkari photo ID ki copy.
  • Aavedan patra — University ya college ke niyam ke hisaab se likhit aavedan, jisme apna naam, course, session, roll number aur registration number likha ho.
  • Jahan verification bhejni hai, uska pata — Agar verification kisi employer ya sansthan ko bhejni hai to uska naam aur pata saaf likhein, taaki university seedha wahan bhej sake.

3. Verification karwane ke steps

  1. Pehle pata karein ki kya chahiye — Jis jagah degree jama karni hai, wahan se pooch lein ki unhe university se seedhi verification chahiye ya aapke dwara di gayi verified copy chalegi.
  2. University/college office se sampark karein — Degree verification ka kaam aam taur par university ke examination ya certificate se jude vibhaag se hota hai. Magadh University ki official website (magadhuniversity.ac.in) par document verification se judi jaankari di gayi hai — wahan se sahi sampark aur prakriya dekhein.
  3. Aavedan aur documents jama karein — Aavedan patra ke saath upar likhe documents ki copy jama karein. Jo bhi shulk ho, uski receipt sambhal kar rakhein.
  4. Naam aur details dobara check karein — Aavedan dene se pehle apna naam, course, session, roll number aur registration number marksheet aur degree se milakar dekhein. Ek chhoti si spelling ki galti bhi verification me deri karwa sakti hai.
  5. Status ka pata karte rahein — Aavedan ke baad university office se sampark me rahein aur poochte rahein ki verification kab tak poori hogi. Agar verification kisi sansthan ko bhejni hai to us sansthan ko bhi bata dein ki aavedan de diya gaya hai.

4. Verification me aam galtiyan — inse bachein

  • Galat naam ya roll number likhna — Aavedan me wahi naam aur number likhein, jo degree aur marksheet par hai. Nickname ya chhota naam na likhein.
  • Online kagaz par bharosa karna — Portal se download ki gayi marksheet sirf jaankari ke liye hoti hai. Verification ke liye aam taur par original degree ya university se jaari kagaz hi maana jata hai.
  • Dalal ya agent ko paise dena — Agar koi vyakti "turant verification" ke naam par online paise maange, to woh dhokha ho sakta hai. Verification sirf university ke official tareeke se hi karwayein.
  • Documents ki copy na rakhna — Aavedan ke saath jo bhi kagaz diye hain, unki photocopy aur receipt apne paas rakhein, taaki baad me koi sawal ho to saboot rahe.
Zaroori baat: Verification ki prakriya, shulk aur samay university ke niyam par nirbhar karta hai aur samay ke saath badal sakta hai. Is article me koi shulk ki rakam ya samay ka dawa nahi kiya gaya hai. Sahi aur aakhri jaankari ke liye magadhuniversity.ac.in dekhein ya university office se sampark karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Kya degree verification online ho sakti hai?
A: Iska jawab university ki vyavastha par nirbhar karta hai. Kuch kaam online aavedan se shuru hote hain, lekin verification aam taur par university ke record se hoti hai. Official website magadhuniversity.ac.in par document verification ki jaankari dekhein ya university office se poochhein.

Q2. Provisional certificate se verification ho sakti hai?
A: Haan, jab tak original degree nahi milti, provisional certificate se bhi kaam chal sakta hai — lekin aakhri faisla us sansthan ka hota hai, jahan aapne kagaz jama kiye hain. Unse pehle hi pooch lein ki provisional certificate chalega ya original degree chahiye.

Q3. Degree par naam ki galti ho to verification se pehle kya karein?
A: Pehle naam ki galti sudharwayein — degree aur marksheet par naam ek jaisa hona chahiye. Galti sudharne ke liye apne college ya university office me aavedan dein. Iske baare me hamari degree certificate guide me bhi bataya gaya hai.

Q4. Verification ke liye kitna samay lagta hai?
A: Iska koi ek niyam nahi hai — ye university ke kaam ke bojh aur prakriya par nirbhar karta hai. Aavedan dene ke baad university office se samay ka anumaan pooch lein aur apne kaam ki last date ko dhyaan me rakhkar samay par aavedan dein.
