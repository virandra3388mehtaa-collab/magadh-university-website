Magadh University Exam Form 2026 — PG 3rd Sem Last Date 28 November, UG 5th Sem 5 December

Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ke Pariksha Niyantrak ne 21 November 2026 ko exam form se judi soochnaayein jaari ki hain. PG 3rd Semester (M.A. / M.Sc. / M.Com., session 2025-27) ke exam form ki aakhri tareekh 28 November 2026 hai, jabki UG 5th Semester (session 2024-28) ka form 5 December 2026 tak bhara ja sakta hai. Dono ki fee, shartein aur form bharne ka tareeka neeche diya gaya hai.

PG 3rd Semester exam form — last date 28 November 2026

Soochna gyapank CEO/1118/2026, dinank 21.11.2026 ke anusaar:

  • Kaun bhar sakta hai: M.A. / M.Sc. / M.Com. (CBCS) 3rd semester, session 2025-27 ke namankit chhatra. Session 2023-25 aur 2024-26 ke 3rd semester me fail ya absent rahe ex-student bhi form bhar sakte hain.
  • Fee: Samanya chhatra Rs 720; SC / ST / BC-1 chhatra Rs 540. Soochna me saaf likha hai ki nirdharit shulk ke alawa koi aur shulk chhatron se nahi liya jaye.
  • Mode: Sirf online — pg.magadhonline.in par PG 2025-27 Applicant Login se, apne User ID aur Password se.
  • Validation zaroori: Form bharne se pehle aapke vibhag ya college ko aapka namankan aur baaki jaankari portal par validate karni hogi — uske baad hi form aur fee online jama ho sakega. Form na khul raha ho to pehle apne vibhag/college se poochhein.
  • Payment non-refundable hai — fee bharne se pehle details ek baar aur milayein.
  • 28 November 2026 ke baad form kisi bhi paristhiti me sweekar nahi hoga — aakhri din ka intezaar mat kijiye.

PG 3rd semester ki theory pariksha 1 se 8 December 2026 tak hai (31 August 2026 ke pariksha karyakram ke anusaar) — CC-10 1 Dec, CC-11 2 Dec, CC-12 4 Dec, CC-13 5 Dec, CC-14 7 Dec aur AECC-2 8 Dec.

UG 5th Semester exam form — last date 5 December 2026

Soochna gyapank CEO/1108/2026, dinank 17.11.2026 ke anusaar UG (CBCS) 5th semester, session 2024-28 ki pariksha ke liye pariksha prapatra aur shulk jama karne ki aakhri tareekh 05.12.2026 hai. Session 2023-27 ke jo chhatra 5th semester me fail ya absent the, wo bhi is baar ex-student ke roop me form bhar sakte hain.

  • Fee: Rs 800 — soochna CEO/1119/2026, dinank 21.11.2026 ke anusaar 5th semester (2024-28) aur 7th semester (2023-27) dono ke liye. Is raashi ke alawa koi shulk nahi liya jayega.
  • 75% attendance ki shart: Pariksha me baithne ke liye 75% varg upasthiti zaroori hai (memo CEO/1104/2026).
  • Practical: 1 se 7 December 2026 tak apne college me.
  • Theory: 10 se 15 December 2026 — MJC-8 10 Dec, MJC-9 11 Dec, MIC-5 12 Dec, MIC-6 14 Dec, Internship 15 Dec. Arts (Social Science/Humanities) subah 10:00–1:00, Science aur Commerce dopahar 2:00–5:00.

UG 7th Semester — fee Rs 800, pariksha 26–30 November se

Soochna CEO/1119/2026 me UG 7th semester (session 2023-27) ki fee bhi Rs 800 likhi hai. 7th semester ki theory pariksha 26 se 30 November 2026 tak hai — MJC-13 26 Nov, MJC-14 27 Nov, MJC-15 28 Nov aur MIC-9 30 Nov. Poori date sheet hamare is article me hai: Magadh University UG 7th Semester Exam 2026.

Exam form kaise bharein — step by step

  1. Official portal kholein: UG ke liye magadhonline.in, PG ke liye pg.magadhonline.in.
  2. Apna section chunein: UG 2024-28 ya PG 2025-27 — apne session ka section kholein.
  3. Login karein: Wahi User ID aur Password daalein jinse namankan hua tha.
  4. Validation check karein: Agar form nahi khul raha hai to pehle apne college ya vibhag se namankan validate karwayein — PG me validation ke bina form nahi khulega.
  5. Form bharein aur fee pay karein: Details dhyan se bharein, fee online jama karein aur receipt/payment proof save karke rakhein.
Savdhaan — fraud se bachein: Soochna me saaf likha hai ki nirdharit pariksha shulk ke alawa koi aur shulk chhatron se nahi liya jaye. Form bharne ke naam par koi extra paise maange to Pariksha Niyantrak karyalay ko batayein. Apna login password ya OTP kisi ke saath share na karein.
