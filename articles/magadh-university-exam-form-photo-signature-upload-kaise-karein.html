Magadh University Exam Form Me Photo Aur Signature Kaise Upload Karein

Exam form ya admission form bharte samay photo aur signature upload karna chhota sa kadam lagta hai, lekin galat photo ya galat signature ki wajah se form ruk sakta hai ya reject bhi ho sakta hai. Admit card par wahi photo chhapti hai jo aap form me upload karte hain, isliye photo saaf aur pehchanne layak honi chahiye. Is guide me saaf photo aur signature taiyaar karne ka tareeka, upload ke steps aur aam galtiyan aasan shabdon me batayi gayi hain. Dhyaan dein: photo aur signature ke size aur format ki sahi jaankari har session ke official notice ya portal par di jati hai — is article me koi size ya format ki rakam nahi likhi gayi hai, kyunki ye har session me badal sakti hai. Upload karne se pehle official notice ya portal par di gayi shartein zaroor padhein.

Sahi photo kaise taiyaar karein

  • Halki aur saaf background chunein — safed ya halke rang ki deewar ke saamne khade hokar photo lein. Peeche koi bheed, poster ya tez rang na ho.
  • Chehra saaf dikhna chahiye — photo me aapka chehra seedha aur poora dikhe; topi, chashme ki chamak ya chehra dhakne wali cheez na ho. Aankhein khuli aur chehra samanya ho.
  • Recent photo use karein — purani photo, jisme aap pehchane na jayein, mat lagayein. Haal hi ki photo hi upload karein.
  • Roshni ka dhyaan rakhein — din ki roshni ya achhi light me photo lein; andhere ya tez flash wali photo blur ho jati hai.
  • Selfie filter mat lagayein — filter, sticker ya beautify mode wali photo official form ke liye theek nahi hoti. Saadhi, bina filter ki photo hi chahiye.
  • Photo ko scan ya crop sahi se karein — agar passport-size photo scan kar rahe hain to use seedha rakhein aur sirf photo wala hissa crop karein; aas-paas ka kagaz ya table na aaye.

Sahi signature kaise taiyaar karein

  • Safed kagaz par kaale pen se karein — saaf safed kagaz par kaale ya neele pen se apne aam signature karein, jaise aap kagaz par karte hain.
  • Signature box ke andar rakhein — signature zyada bada ya bahut chhota na ho; use kagaz ke beech me saaf tareeke se karein.
  • Scan ya photo lein — signature wale kagaz ki saaf photo lein ya scan karein, phir sirf signature wala hissa crop karein.
  • Naam ko signature ki jagah mat likhein — signature ki jagah apna naam saaf aksharon me likhna theek nahi hota; wahi signature karein jo aap hamesha karte hain aur jo aapke documents se milta ho.
  • Ek hi signature har jagah rakhein — form, admit card aur exam hall me aapka signature ek jaisa hona chahiye, isliye wahi signature use karein jo aapne pehle ke documents me kiya hai.

Photo aur signature upload karne ke steps

  1. Official notice padhein — form bharne se pehle us session ke official notice me photo aur signature ke size, format aur shartein padhein. Wahi shartein aakhri mani jati hain.
  2. Photo aur signature pehle se taiyaar rakhein — form bharne se pehle dono files apne phone ya computer me taiyaar karke rakhein, taaki form bharte samay jaldbazi na ho.
  3. Student portal par login karein — magadhonline.in par apne user id aur password se login karein.
  4. Form kholein aur upload wale hisse par jayein — exam form ya admission form me photo aur signature upload karne ka hissa milega; wahan "Choose File" ya "Upload" jaisa vikalp dikhega.
  5. File chunein aur preview dekhein — photo aur signature ki file chunein. Upload hone ke baad preview me dekhein ki photo seedhi hai, chehra saaf dikh raha hai aur signature poora dikh raha hai.
  6. Galat ho to dobara upload karein — preview me kuch galat lage to submit karne se pehle dobara sahi file upload karein. Submit ke baad sudhaar mushkil hota hai.
  7. Form submit karein aur receipt bachayein — saari details aur preview check karne ke baad hi form submit karein. Submit ke baad form ki copy ya receipt save karke rakhein.
Zaroori baat: Photo aur signature ke size aur format ki rakam har session me badal sakti hai, isliye is article me koi rakam nahi likhi gayi hai. Upload karne se pehle magadhuniversity.ac.in ke official notice ya magadhonline.in par portal me di gayi shartein zaroor padhein. Agar file upload nahi ho rahi, to pehle wahi shartein check karein.

Upload fail ho jaye to kya karein

  • File ka size aur format check karein — agar file upload nahi ho rahi, to official notice me bataya gaya format aur size dekhein. Galat format ki file upload nahi hoti.
  • Photo ko dobara scan karein — blur ya tedhi photo ko dobara achhi roshni me lekar upload karein.
  • Internet aur browser check karein — kamzor internet par badi file upload nahi hoti. Achhe internet par dobara koshish karein aur browser ko refresh karein.
  • College ya portal support se sampark karein — baar-baar fail hone par apne college ke office se poochhein ya magadhonline.in par di gayi support email par sampark karein. Apni samasya me session aur form ka naam likhein.

Aam galtiyan — in se bachein

  • Dusre ki photo lagana — kisi aur ki photo lagana galat hai aur form reject ho sakta hai. Hamesha apni hi photo lagayein.
  • Blur ya andheri photo — andhere me li gayi ya hilti hui photo admit card par pehchani nahi jayegi.
  • Preview dekhe bina submit karna — upload ke baad preview na dekhna sabse aam galti hai. Preview me hi pata chalta hai ki photo tedhi to nahi hai.
  • Galat file upload karna — photo ki jagah signature ya signature ki jagah photo upload karne se form me gadbad hoti hai. Upload ke baad dono ka preview alag se check karein.
  • Aakhri din par form bharna — aakhri din portal par bheed hoti hai; photo-signature ki gadbad sudharne ka samay nahi milta. Form samay se pehle bhar lein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Photo aur signature ka size aur format kahan se pata chalega?
A: Har session ke official notice ya portal par form bharte samay size aur format bataya jata hai. Is article me koi rakam nahi likhi gayi hai — sahi jaankari ke liye magadhuniversity.ac.in ka notice ya magadhonline.in par form ka upload wala hissa dekhein.

Q2. Kya mobile se li gayi photo upload kar sakte hain?
A: Haan, agar photo saaf hai, roshni achhi hai aur chehra seedha dikh raha hai to mobile se li gayi photo bhi chalti hai. Lekin filter ya sticker wali photo mat lagayein aur upload se pehle preview zaroor dekhein.

Q3. Upload ke baad photo galat dikh rahi hai to kya karein?
A: Form submit karne se pehle dobara sahi file upload karke preview check karein. Submit ke baad sudhaar mushkil hota hai, isliye submit se pehle hi sab kuch check kar lein.

Q4. Signature admit card par kyun zaroori hai?
A: Admit card par aapki photo aur signature se exam hall me aapki pehchan hoti hai. Isliye signature wahi hona chahiye jo aap aam taur par karte hain aur jo aapke documents se milta ho.
