Magadh University Registration Card Kaise Download Karein — Steps Aur Zaroori Baatein

Magadh University me admission ke baad har student ko ek registration card milta hai — ye woh document hai jisme aapka registration number likha hota hai. Ye number aapki pehchan hai: exam form bharte samay, admit card download karte samay, result aur marksheet dekhte samay har jagah yahi registration number manga jata hai. Bina valid registration ke student semester exam me baithne ke liye eligible nahi hota — isliye registration card ko sambhal kar rakhna aur us par likhi jaankari ko shuru me hi check kar lena bahut zaroori hai. Is guide me UG aur PG dono ke liye registration card pane ke steps, card par kya-kya likha hota hai aur galti dikhe to kya karna hai — ye sab apne shabdon me samjhaya gaya hai. Registration kab aur kis session ke liye live hai, ye har baar official portal par hi dekhein — is article me koi date ya session ka dawa nahi kiya gaya hai, kyunki ye har session ke saath badalta rehta hai.

Registration card par kya-kya likha hota hai

  • Registration number — aapka unique number, jo har exam aur document me kaam aata hai.
  • Registration ki date aur form number — kab register hue aur admission form ka number.
  • Student ka naam, pita ka naam, course aur session — jaise B.A. (session 2024–28) ya M.A. (session 2026–28).
  • College / department ka naam — jahan aap padh rahe hain.
  • Mobile number aur email ID — jo admission ke samay diye gaye the.
  • Controller of Examinations ke hastakshar — card ki authenticity ke liye.

Card milte hi ye saari jaankari apne Aadhaar aur admission form se mila kar check karein. Naam ki spelling ya koi number galat ho to use turant theek karwayein — baad me exam form ya admit card par wahi galti chhap jati hai.

UG students: registration card kaise payein

  1. Official student portal kholein: Apne browser me magadhonline.in kholein. Kisi bhi dusri site, WhatsApp forward ya agent ke link se login mat karein.
  2. Apna session/course chunein: Portal par apne course aur session (jaise U.G. 2024–28) se juda section chunein. Har session ka panel alag hota hai.
  3. Login karein: Apni user ID aur password se login karein. Ye details aam taur par admission ke samay college se milti hain.
  4. Registration card ka option dhoondhein: Login ke baad "Registration Card" ya is se milta-julta option chunein. Card wahan PDF ke roop me milega to use download karke print nikaal lein.
  5. Portal par na mile to college se sampark karein: Kai colleges me registration card college ke login se print karke students ko diya jata hai. Agar portal par card na dikhe to apne college ke exam section ya office se poochhein — wahan se card ki print copy mil jayegi.
  6. Ek extra copy surakshit rakhein: Card ki PDF ki ek copy apne phone ya email me bhi save karke rakhein, taaki print kho jaye to dobara nikaal sakein.

PG students: registration kaise poora karein (pg.magadhonline.in)

PG admission ke liye university ke official instruction document ke anusaar registration ki prakriya is prakar hai:

  1. Portal kholein: pg.magadhonline.in kholein aur apni user ID aur password se login karein.
  2. Offer letter download karein: Login ke baad "Download Offer Letter" par click karke offer letter ka print nikalein aur apne college ya department me jama karke document verification karwayein.
  3. Dobara login karke Registration chunein: Verification ke baad dobara login karein — screen par "Registration" ka button dikhega, use click karein aur "Register Now" chunein.
  4. Fees jama karein: Payment gateway par jaakar registration fees ka bhugtan karein. Official instruction ke anusaar fees university ke niyam aur aapki category ke hisab se ₹600 ya ₹450 hoti hai — apne session ki sahi rakam hamesha portal ya official notice par hi dekhein, kyunki ye badal sakti hai.
  5. Registration payment slip download karein: Payment safal hone par registration payment slip generate hogi — use download karke sambhal kar rakhein. Iske saath aapki admission-cum-registration prakriya poori ho jati hai.

Registration number bhool gaye ya card me galti ho to kya karein

  • Registration number bhool gaye hon to pehle apne admission form, fees ki receipt ya college se mile kisi bhi document par dekhein — number wahan likha hota hai. Na mile to college office se poochhein.
  • Naam, pita ka naam ya kisi number me galti ho to turant college ke exam section ko likhit roop me batayein. Galti ka sudhaar university se hota hai, isliye jitni jaldi batayenge utna behtar hoga — exam form bharne se pehle sudhaar ho jana chahiye.
  • Password bhool gaye hon to portal par password wapas lene ka tareeka dekhein, ya college se sampark karein — login details kisi anjaan vyakti se share mat karein.
  • Registration abhi tak hua hi nahi ho to pehle ye jaanein ki aapka admission aur document verification poora hua hai ya nahi. Verification ke bina registration aage nahi badhta, isliye college se apni sthiti poochhna sabse pehla kadam hai.
Zaroori baat: Registration card ke naam par koi bhi vyakti agar paise mange, aapki login details mange ya "card banwa dunga" kahe, to woh farzi hai. Registration sirf official portal aur college ke zariye hota hai — apni user ID aur password kisi se share nahi karni chahiye.

Registration card se jude kaam — ek jhalak

  • Exam form: form bharte samay registration number dena padta hai — exam form fees kaise jama karein padhein.
  • Admit card: admit card par registration number chhapa hota hai — admit card kaise download karein padhein.
  • Result aur marksheet: result dekhne ke liye bhi registration/roll number chahiye — marksheet kaise download karein padhein.
  • Documents: admission aur exam ke liye kaun se documents chahiye — documents ki poori list padhein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Registration card kahan se milega?
A: UG students ko magadhonline.in student portal se ya apne college se milta hai; PG students pg.magadhonline.in par login karke registration prakriya poori karte hain aur payment slip download karte hain.

Q2. Registration number kyun zaroori hai?
A: Exam form, admit card, result aur marksheet — har jagah registration number chahiye hota hai. Ye aapki university me pehchan hai, isliye ise sambhal kar rakhein.

Q3. Registration fees kitni hai?
A: PG ke official instruction document ke anusaar fees category ke hisab se ₹600 ya ₹450 hoti hai, aur UG me registration aur exam fees alag-alag notice se tay hoti hain. Apne session ki sahi rakam hamesha official portal ya notice par hi dekhein.

Q4. Card par naam galat chhap gaya to kya karein?
A: Exam form bharne se pehle college ke exam section ko likhit roop me batayein aur sudhaar ki prakriya poochhein. Der karne par wahi galti admit card aur marksheet par bhi aa jayegi.

Q5. Registration card kho gaya to kya karein?
A: Portal se dobara download karke print nikalein. Portal par na mile to college office se duplicate copy ke liye sampark karein. Isi liye PDF ki ek copy pehle se save karke rakhna behtar rehta hai.
