Magadh University PG 3rd Semester Exam 2026 — 1 December se Date Sheet, Admit Card, Practical

Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ke M.A. / M.Sc. / M.Com. (CBCS) 3rd Semester, session 2025-27 ke chhatron ki theory pariksha 1 December 2026 se shuru ho rahi hai. Pariksha Niyantrak ne pariksha karyakram gyapank CEO/970/2026, dinank 31.08.2026 se jaari kiya tha. Is article me paper-wise date sheet, dono group ki sitting time, practical aur viva ki jaankari aur admit card se judi zaroori baatein di gayi hain.

3 December aur 6 December ko koi paper nahi hai — theory pariksha 8 December 2026 ko AECC-2 ke saath khatm hogi.

Group A aur Group B — sitting time aur vishay

PG ke vishay do group me bante hain, aur dono group ki pariksha ek hi din, alag sitting me hoti hai:

  • Group A — subah 10:00 se 1:00: English, Rajneeti Vigyan (Pol. Sc.), Manovigyan (Psychology), Zoology, Lok Prashasan (Pub. Adm.), Electronics, Chemistry, Commerce, Geography, Physics.
  • Group B — dopahar 2:00 se 5:00: Home Science, Economics, Philosophy, History, Hindi, Urdu, Sanskrit, Persian, Botany, Pali, Mathematics, Buddhist Studies, A.I. & A.S., Sociology, L.S.W., Maghi.
  • AECC-2 (8 December): Arts ke chhatra subah wali sitting me aur Science / Commerce ke chhatra dopahar wali sitting me pariksha denge.

Apna vishay kis group me hai, ye upar wali list se mila lijiye — pariksha kendra par galat sitting me jaane se bachein.

Practical, viva-voce aur group discussion — 1 December se pehle

Viva-voce, group discussion aur practical pariksha 1 December 2026 se pehle apne vibhag ya college me honge. Inki sahi tareekh aur samay aapke vibhag/college se milega — practical ki tareekh apne college se aaj hi pakki kar lijiye, kyunki theory shuru hone ke baad inke liye samay nahi milega.

Exam form — aaj 28 November aakhri din

PG 3rd semester ke exam form aur fee jama karne ki aakhri tareekh aaj, 28 November 2026 hai (soochna CEO/1118/2026, 21.11.2026). Fee samanya chhatra ke liye Rs 720 aur SC / ST / BC-1 chhatra ke liye Rs 540 hai. Iske baad form kisi bhi paristhiti me sweekar nahi hoga. Fee, validation aur form bharne ke steps ki poori jaankari hamare is article me hai: Magadh University Exam Form 2026 — PG 3rd Sem Last Date 28 November, UG 5th Sem 5 December.

Admit card kab aayega?

Pariksha karyakram (CEO/970/2026) me admit card jaari hone ki koi tareekh nahi di gayi hai, isliye iski koi tareekh hum yahan nahi likh rahe. Admit card ke liye pg.magadhonline.in (PG 2025-27 section) aur apne college ka notice board roz check karte rahein. Admit card milte hi us par apna naam, vishay, pariksha kendra aur sitting time mila lijiye — koi galti dikhe to turant apne college ya vibhag ko batayein.

Exam se pehle ye kaam kar lijiye

  1. Admit card aur pehchan patra: Admit card ke saath ek original ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID) le jayein.
  2. Sitting time pakka karein: Apne vishay ka group (A ya B) upar se dekh lein aur usi samay kendra par pahunchein.
  3. Pariksha kendra: Kendra admit card par ya official soochna me milega — official website magadhuniversity.ac.in par check karein.
  4. Walkout par dobara pariksha nahi: Karyakram me saaf likha hai ki walkout ya stay-out ki sthiti me dobara pariksha nahi hogi.
Savdhaan — fraud se bachein: "Paper leak" ya "pehle se question paper" ke naam par paise maangne wale group aur message fake hote hain. Aise kisi ko paise na dein aur shikayat ke liye cybercrime helpline 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in ka upyog karein. Apna login password ya OTP kisi ke saath share na karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University PG 3rd semester exam 2026 kab se hai?
A: Theory pariksha 1 se 8 December 2026 tak hai (gyapank CEO/970/2026, 31.08.2026).

Q2. PG 3rd sem ke paper kis tareekh ko hain?
A: CC-10 1 Dec, CC-11 2 Dec, CC-12 4 Dec, CC-13 5 Dec, CC-14 7 Dec aur AECC-2 8 Dec 2026.

Q3. PG exam ki timing kya hai?
A: Group A (10 vishay) subah 10:00–1:00 aur Group B (18 vishay) dopahar 2:00–5:00. AECC-2 me Arts subah aur Science/Commerce dopahar.

Q4. PG 3rd sem ka admit card kab aayega?
A: Pariksha karyakram me admit card ki tareekh nahi di gayi hai. pg.magadhonline.in aur apne college ka notice board dekhte rahein.

Q5. PG 3rd sem exam form ki last date kya hai?
A: 28 November 2026 (CEO/1118/2026). Fee Rs 720 (samanya) / Rs 540 (SC/ST/BC-1).
