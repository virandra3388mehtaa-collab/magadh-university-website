Magadh University Exam Pattern Aur Marking Scheme Kaise Samjhein — Poori Guide

Magadh University ke chaar saal ke UG programme (NEP) me har paper 100 marks ka hota hai — 30 marks college ki Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) se aur 70 marks university ki End Semester Exam (ESE) se milte hain. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki 30 marks ka batwara kaise hota hai, ESE kaise hoti hai, pass hone ke liye kitne marks chahiye, grade table kya hai, SGPA kaise nikalta hai aur fail ho jayein to kya hota hai. Is article me ank aur niyam wahi likhe gaye hain jo university ke CBCS niyam aur bharosemand jaankari me diye gaye hain — kisi bhi ek article ko aakhri na manen, apne college aur official notice se confirm karna hi sahi hoga.

CIA 30 Marks Ka Batwara — College Kya Dekhta Hai?

CIA ke 30 marks teen hisson me bante hain — mid-semester written test 15 marks, seminar/quiz/presentation ya assignment 10 marks, aur attendance aur conduct 5 marks. Matlab aapke semester ke 30 marks sirf ek test se nahi, poore semester ke kaam se bante hain. Mid-semester test me achha karna, assignment samay par jama karna aur class me niyamit rehna — teeno ka seedha asar aapke kul marks par padta hai. CIA ke marks aapka college deta hai aur university ko bhejta hai, isliye in marks ke baare me koi sawal ho to apne department ya teacher se hi poochhein.

ESE 70 Marks — University Ki End Semester Exam

ESE 70 marks ki hoti hai aur use university conduct karti hai — iska programme (date sheet) university ki official notice se aata hai. Exam programme me har paper ki tareekh aur samay likha hota hai; aam taur par din me do sittings hoti hain — subah 10 baje se 1 baje tak aur dopahar 2 baje se 5 baje tak. Apne semester ka exam programme aur centre ki jaankari admit card aur official notice se hi lein — is website ki date sheet guide me programme dekhne ke steps diye gaye hain.

Course Ke Prakar — MJC, MIC, MDC, AEC, SEC, VAC Kya Hain?

Grade card par har paper apne course type ke saath likha hota hai — MJC (Major Course), MIC (Minor Course), MDC (Multidisciplinary Course), AEC (Ability Enhancement Course), SEC (Skill Enhancement Course) aur VAC (Value-Added Course). MJC aapka mukhya subject hota hai, MIC doosra (minor) subject, aur baaki courses NEP ke hisaab se alag-alag kaushal aur vishay sikhate hain. Har paper ke CIA aur ESE marks aur letter grade alag-alag dikhaye jate hain, isliye marksheet padhte waqt pehle yeh samajh lein ki kaun sa paper kis type ka hai — isse SGPA samajhna bhi aasan ho jata hai.

Passing Marks Aur Grade Table — Kitne Marks Par Pass?

Har paper me pass hone ke liye kam se kam P grade chahiye — 45 percent se kam marks par F grade (Fail) milta hai. Grade aapke kul marks (CIA + ESE) ke percentage par milta hai. Neeche woh grade diye gaye hain jo bharosemand jaankari me saaf likhe gaye hain:

Beech ke grade (jaise A aur B) bhi isi tarah percentage ke hisaab se milte hain — unki sahi seema aapki marksheet aur university ke niyam me di hoti hai, isliye grade samajhne ke liye apni marksheet hi dekhein.

SGPA Kaise Nikalta Hai?

SGPA har subject ke credit ko uske grade point se guna karke, sabka jod karke aur kul credits se bhaag dekar nikalta hai — yeh ek semester ke overall pradarshan ka credit-weighted average hota hai. Har paper ka credit aur grade point marksheet par likha hota hai. Seedha formula hai: SGPA = (sabhi subjects ke credit × grade point ka jod) ÷ (sabhi subjects ke credits ka jod). SGPA ki poori samajh aur udaharan ke liye is website ki SGPA guide padhein, aur apna SGPA nikalne ke liye Student Portal par diya gaya SGPA calculator istemal kar sakte hain — lekin aakhri aur sahi SGPA wahi hai jo aapki official marksheet par likha hai.

Fail Ho Gaye To Kya Hoga?

CBCS niyam ke anusaar koi supplementary exam nahi hoti — fail hua paper us semester ki agli End Semester Exam me clear hota hai, aur usme aapke purane CIA marks bane rehte hain, sirf ESE dobara deni hoti hai. Iska matlab yeh hai ki agar aapka CIA achha tha to woh bekar nahi jata — agle mauke par sirf university wali exam dobara deni hoti hai. Fail hone par turant apne college se baat karein aur agle ESE ka programme aur form ki jaankari lein. Is website ki back paper guide me iski poori jaankari di gayi hai.

PG Ka Exam Pattern — Kya Alag Hai?

PG (2 saal, CBCS) me bhi internal assessment aur end-semester exam, dono hote hain — lekin unke marks ka batwara UG jaisa ho, yeh zaroori nahi hai. PG ke har paper ke marks aur pattern ke liye apne PG syllabus aur university ki notice hi dekhein; is article me PG ke ank isliye nahi likhe gaye hain kyunki unke baare me bharosemand aur ek-jaisi jaankari nahi mili. PG admission aur Samarth portal ki jaankari is website ki PG admission guide me di gayi hai.

Practical Ke Marks Kaise Milte Hain?

Practical subjects me marks practical me kiye gaye kaam, practical file aur viva, teeno ko dekhkar milte hain — practical exam theory se pehle aur apne college me hota hai. Practical ke marks ka batwara har subject ke hisaab se alag hota hai, isliye apne subject ke pattern ke liye apne department se poochh lein. Practical aur viva ki poori jaankari is website ki practical exam guide me di gayi hai.

Exam Me Achhe Marks Ke Liye Aasan Tareeke

  1. CIA ko halke me na lein — 30 marks poore semester se bante hain; mid-semester test, assignment aur attendance, teeno par dhyan dein.
  2. Attendance 75% se upar rakhein — university ki exam notice me admit card ke liye 75% attendance ki shart likhi jati hai, aur attendance ke 5 marks bhi CIA me judte hain. Attendance calculator se samajh sakte hain ki kitni class aur chahiye.
  3. Grade table ko samajhkar padhein — 45% pass ki seema hai, isliye kamzor subject me bhi CIA ke marks aapko pass kara sakte hain; har paper ke marks par dhyan dein.
  4. Exam programme aate hi date sheet bana lein — kaun sa paper kab hai, yeh pehle se jaan lena taiyaari ko aasan karta hai.
  5. Marksheet aate hi SGPA aur grade check karein — koi galti lage to turant college se baat karein; marksheet correction ki guide is website par hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University UG me har paper kitne marks ka hota hai?
A: Har paper 100 marks ka hota hai — 30 marks college ki CIA (internal assessment) se aur 70 marks university ki End Semester Exam (ESE) se milte hain.

Q2. CIA ke 30 marks kaise bante hain?
A: Mid-semester written test 15 marks, seminar/quiz/presentation ya assignment 10 marks, aur attendance aur conduct 5 marks — in teeno se milkar 30 marks bante hain.

Q3. Magadh University me pass hone ke liye kitne percent chahiye?
A: Har paper me kam se kam P grade chahiye — 45 percent se kam marks par F grade (Fail) milta hai.

Q4. SGPA kaise nikalta hai?
A: Har subject ke credit ko uske grade point se guna karein, sabka jod karein aur kul credits se bhaag dein. Poori samajh ke liye is website ki SGPA guide padhein.

Q5. Fail ho gaye to kya supplementary exam hoti hai?
A: Nahi — CBCS niyam ke anusaar supplementary exam nahi hoti. Fail hua paper us semester ki agli ESE me clear hota hai, aur purane CIA marks bane rehte hain, sirf ESE dobara deni hoti hai.
