Magadh University PG 3rd Semester 2025-27: Naamankan Soochi 23 November Tak Jama — Students Ke Liye Kya Matlab Hai?

Magadh University ke Dean Student's Welfare (अध्यक्ष, छात्र कल्याण) ne 20 November 2026 ko ek notice jaari kiya hai — PG session 2025-27 ke 3rd Semester me naamankit students ki updated subject-wise soochi, online aur offline dono tareekon se naamankit students ki, 23 November 2026 tak DSW office me jama karni hai. Ye notice university ki official website par 21 November 2026 ko dikha. Is article me bataya gaya hai ki notice me kya likha hai, ye kiske liye hai, last date kya hai aur PG 3rd Semester ke students ko kya karna chahiye.

Ye Notice Kya Hai?

Ye "नामांकन संबंधी सूचना" hai — memo number D.S.W/173/26, dinank 20.11.2026, Dean Student's Welfare, Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ki taraf se. Notice me kaha gaya hai ki PG vibhaagon aur angibhoot mahavidyalayon (jahan PG ki padhai hoti hai) me session 2025-27 ke 3rd Semester me offline aur online naamankit students ka updated, subject-wise vistrit byora DSW ke karyalaya me jama karna hai. Notice me is kaam ko "अतिआवश्यक" kaha gaya hai — matlab ise turant aur zaroor poora karna hai.

Ye Notice Kiske Liye Hai?

Ye notice seedhe taur par PG vibhaagon ke vibhagadhyakshon aur angibhoot/alpsankhyak mahavidyalayon ke pracharyon ko bheja gaya hai — soochi jama karne ki zimmedari unki hai, students ki nahi. Lekin iska asar PG session 2025-27 ke 3rd Semester ke un sabhi students par padta hai jinka naamankan offline ya online tareeke se hua hai. Agar aap PG 2025-27 ke 3rd Semester ke student hain, to ye notice aapke enrolment record se juda hai — isiliye aapko ye jaanna zaroori hai ki aapka naam updated soochi me hai ya nahi.

Naamankan Soochi Jama Karne Ki Last Date Kya Hai?

Last date 23 November 2026 hai — is din tak naamankit students ka subject-wise vistrit byora DSW ke karyalaya me jama karna hai. Notice 20 November 2026 ko jaari hua aur official website par 21 November 2026 ko dikha, isliye vibhaagon aur colleges ke paas soochi taiyaar karne ke liye sirf kuch din hain. Students ke liye matlab ye hai ki 23 November se pehle apne vibhaag ya college se confirm kar lein ki aapka naam soochi me shaamil hai.

Online Aur Offline Naamankan Ka Kya Matlab Hai?

Notice me bataya gaya hai ki pehle do karyalaya gyapankon — D.S.W/122/26 dinank 29.08.2026 aur D.S.W/131/26 dinank 11.09.2026 — ke dwara online naamankan ke nirdesh diye gaye the, lekin kai vibhaagon aur mahavidyalayon ne offline naamankan bhi liya hai. Isiliye ab dono tareekon — online aur offline — se naamankit students ka alag-alag nahi, balki ek saath updated aur subject-wise byora manga gaya hai. Matlab, chahe aapka admission online hua ho ya college me jakar offline form bharkar, aapka naam is ek soochi me hona chahiye.

PG 3rd Semester Ke Students Ko Kya Karna Chahiye?

Students ko koi form nahi bharna hai — bas ye 3 kaam 23 November se pehle kar lein: (1) apne vibhaag ya college ke office se poochhein ki aapka naam updated subject-wise naamankan soochi me hai ya nahi, (2) agar aapka admission offline hua tha to apni admission receipt aur registration ki details saath rakhein, (3) naam na mile ya koi galti dikhe to turant vibhagadhyaksh ya pracharya ko batayein. Jo students PG vibhaag me padhte hain, woh apne vibhaag se poochhein; jo college me padhte hain, woh apne college office se. Ye jaanch abhi kar lena behtar hai, kyunki soochi jama hone ke baad usme naam judwana mushkil ho sakta hai.

Naam Soochi Me Na Ho To Kya Hoga?

Naamankan soochi hi aapke enrolment ka official record hoti hai — agar aapka naam is soochi me chhoot gaya to aage exam form ya admit card se judi jaankari me dikkat aa sakti hai, isiliye 23 November se pehle apne vibhaag se confirm kar lena sabse surakshit tareeka hai. Notice me students ke liye koi jurmana ya saza nahi likhi hai — ye notice vibhaagon aur colleges ko soochi jama karne ka nirdesh hai. Lekin record me naam na hone par aapko baad me apna enrolment sabit karne ke liye admission receipt aur college ke record par nirbhar rehna padega, isliye pehle se hi naam ki pushti kar lein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. PG 3rd Semester 2025-27 ki naamankan soochi kab tak jama karni hai?
A: 23 November 2026 tak — naamankit students ka subject-wise vistrit byora Dean Student's Welfare ke karyalaya me jama karna hai.

Q2. Kya ye notice naye admission ke liye hai?
A: Nahi. Ye notice pehle se naamankit students ki updated subject-wise soochi ke baare me hai. Isse naya admission nahi khul raha hai aur students ko koi naya form nahi bharna hai.

Q3. Main PG 3rd Semester (2025-27) ka student hoon — mujhe kya karna hai?
A: Apne vibhaag ya college se confirm karein ki aapka naam updated naamankan soochi me hai. Offline admission liya ho to apni admission receipt saath rakhein aur 23 November se pehle ye jaanch poori kar lein.

Q4. Ye notice kisne aur kab jaari kiya?
A: Dean Student's Welfare (अध्यक्ष, छात्र कल्याण), Magadh University, Bodh Gaya — memo D.S.W/173/26, dinank 20 November 2026. Official website par ye notice 21 November 2026 ko dikha.
