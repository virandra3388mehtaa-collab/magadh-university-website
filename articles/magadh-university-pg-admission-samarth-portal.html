Magadh University PG Admission Kaise Lein — Samarth Portal Guide

Graduation ke baad kai students Magadh University se PG (postgraduate) karna chahte hain — jaise M.A., M.Sc. ya M.Com. University ki official website par PG admission ke liye Samarth Portal par online aavedan ki jaankari di gayi hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki Samarth portal par aavedan kaise karein, kaun se documents chahiye aur merit list ki jaankari kahan milegi. Ye sab apne shabdon me likha gaya hai; admission ki dates, fees aur merit list ki aakhri jaankari hamesha official notice se hi lein.

PG admission kis portal par hota hai

  • Samarth Portal — Magadh University ki official website par PG (2026-2028) ke liye Samarth Portal par online admission ki jaankari di gayi hai. Aavedan online hi karna hota hai.
  • Official website se hi aavedan karein — admission ka link aur jaankari magadhuniversity.ac.in par dekhein. Kisi aur website ya vyakti ke dwara diye gaye link par bharosa mat karein.
  • Dates aur fees official notice se lein — admission kab se shuru hoga, last date kya hai aur fees kitni hai, ye har saal official notice se tay hota hai. Is article me koi date ya fees nahi likhi gayi hai.

Samarth portal par aavedan karne ke aam steps

  1. Official website par jayein — magadhuniversity.ac.in par PG admission se judi jaankari aur Samarth Portal ka link dekhein.
  2. Samarth portal par register karein — pehli baar aavedan kar rahe hain to apna naam, mobile number aur email se naya account banayein. Apna password safe jagah likh kar rakhein.
  3. Apni details bharein — form me apna naam, graduation ka course, session, university aur marks sahi-sahi likhein. Details apni marksheet se milakar bharein.
  4. Course aur college chunein — jis subject me PG karna hai aur jis college ya department me admission chahiye, use dhyaan se chunein. Ek baar form jama ho jaye to badlav mushkil ho sakta hai.
  5. Documents upload karein — zaroori documents ki scan copy upload karein. Documents ki list neeche di gayi hai.
  6. Fees jama karein aur form jama karein — aavedan ki fees official tareeke se hi jama karein aur form jama karne ke baad uski raseed ya print apne paas rakhein.

PG admission ke liye zaroori documents

  • Graduation ki marksheet aur degree/provisional certificate (agar mil gaya ho)
  • 10th aur 12th ki marksheet
  • Migration certificate (agar dusri university se graduation kiya ho)
  • Photo ID — Aadhaar card ya koi sarkari ID
  • Passport size photo
  • Caste certificate (agar aarakshan ka labh lena ho)
  • Income certificate (agar scholarship ke liye chahiye)
  • Ye ek aam list hai — sahi list ke liye admission notice aur Samarth portal par di gayi jaankari hi dekhein. Documents ki poori taiyaari ke liye documents guide bhi dekh sakte hain.

Merit list aur admission ki jaankari kaise milegi

  • Merit list official website par aati hai — aavedan ke baad merit list magadhuniversity.ac.in ya Samarth portal par jaari hoti hai. Merit list me apna naam aur allotted college check karein.
  • Document verification — merit list me naam aane ke baad documents ki jaanch (verification) ke liye college ya department jana pad sakta hai. Original documents saath le jayein.
  • Fees aur admission ki last date ka dhyaan rakhein — merit list me naam aane ke baad admission lene ki bhi ek last date hoti hai. Der ho jaye to seat kisi aur ko mil sakti hai, isliye notice par nazar rakhein.
  • College se sampark rakhein — jis college me admission mila hai, uske office se aage ki prakriya poochh lein.
Zaroori baat: Admission ki dates, fees aur merit list har saal badal sakti hai. Is article me koi date, fees ya merit list ka dawa nahi kiya gaya hai — aakhri jaankari ke liye magadhuniversity.ac.in par aaya official notice aur Samarth portal hi dekhein. Admission dilwane ka vaada karke paise mangne wale vyakti se savdhaan rahein.

Aavedan karte samay aam galtiyan

  • Galat marks ya session likhna — form bharne ke baad saari details marksheet se milakar check karein. Galat jaankari se aavedan radd ho sakta hai.
  • Documents ki saaf copy na upload karna — dhundhli ya adhuri scan copy se form atak sakta hai; saaf scan upload karein.
  • Last date ka intezaar karna — aakhri din portal par bheed ya technical pareshani ho sakti hai; aavedan pehle hi kar lein.
  • Form ki copy na rakhna — jama kiye gaye form aur fees ki raseed ki print ya screenshot apne paas zaroor rakhein.
  • Galat course ya college chunna — form jama karne se pehle course aur college ka chunav dobara check karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University me PG admission kis portal par hota hai?
A: Official website par PG admission ke liye Samarth Portal par online aavedan ki jaankari di gayi hai. Admission ka link aur steps magadhuniversity.ac.in par dekhein.

Q2. PG admission ki last date aur fees kya hai?
A: Ye har saal official notice se tay hota hai, isliye is article me koi date ya fees nahi likhi gayi hai. Aakhri jaankari official notice aur Samarth portal se hi lein.

Q3. Merit list kahan dekhein?
A: Merit list official website magadhuniversity.ac.in ya Samarth portal par jaari hoti hai. Apna naam aur allotted college wahin check karein.

Q4. Graduation dusri university se kiya hai to kya admission milega?
A: Aam taur par haan, lekin iske liye migration certificate ki zaroorat pad sakti hai. Migration certificate ka tareeka is guide me bataya gaya hai. Sahi niyam admission notice se confirm karein.
