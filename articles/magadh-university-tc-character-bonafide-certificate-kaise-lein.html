Magadh University Se TC, Character Certificate Aur Bonafide Certificate Kaise Lein

College ki padhai ke dauraan ya uske baad kai baar teen kagazat maange jate hain — Transfer Certificate (TC), Character Certificate aur Bonafide Certificate. Ye teeno alag kaam ke liye hote hain aur teeno aapke college se hi milte hain, university se nahi. Kai students inme antar nahi samajhte aur galat certificate ke liye aavedan kar dete hain, jisse samay barbaad hota hai. Is guide me teeno ka matlab, kab kaun sa chahiye aur aavedan ka tareeka bataya gaya hai. Shulk aur samay ki jaankari har college me alag ho sakti hai, isliye aakhri jaankari apne college office se hi confirm karein.

1. Transfer Certificate (TC) — kya hai aur kab chahiye

  • Kya hai — Transfer Certificate woh kagaz hai, jo batata hai ki aapne ek college se apni padhai poori ki ya wahan se transfer liya. Isme aapka naam, course, session aur college chhodne ki tareekh likhi hoti hai.
  • Kab chahiye — Jab aap kisi doosre college ya university me admission lete hain, khaas taur par PG admission ya doosri university me transfer ke samay, to naya sansthan TC maangta hai. Ye is baat ka saboot hai ki aap pichhle sansthan se vidhiwat roop se alag hue hain.
  • Kahan se milta hai — Aapke purane college ke office se. Course poora hone ke baad aavedan karne par college TC jaari karta hai.
  • Kaise aavedan karein — College office me ek likhit aavedan dein, jisme apna naam, course, session, roll number aur TC lene ka kaaran likhein. Aavedan ke saath aam taur par admission receipt ya ID proof ki copy aur marksheet ki copy maangi jati hai. College ki prakriya ke hisaab se TC kuch din me taiyaar hota hai.
  • Dhyaan rakhein — TC aam taur par ek hi baar original roop me milta hai. Milte hi uski kai photocopy aur ek scanned copy sambhal kar rakhein. Agar TC kho jaye to duplicate ke liye alag prakriya hoti hai, jo samay legi — isliye original ko surakshit jagah rakhein.

2. Character Certificate — kya hai aur kab chahiye

  • Kya hai — Character Certificate college ke principal dwara jaari kiya jane wala kagaz hai, jo batata hai ki college me aapka aacharan aur vyavhaar kaisa raha. Isme aam taur par likha hota hai ki student ka character achha hai aur college me uske khilaf koi shikayat nahi rahi.
  • Kab chahiye — PG admission, sarkari naukri ke aavedan, aur kai baar scholarship ya doosre sansthan me admission ke samay character certificate maanga jata hai.
  • Kahan se milta hai — Aapke college se hi, principal ke hastakshar ke saath. Iske liye bhi college office me likhit aavedan dena hota hai.
  • Kaise aavedan karein — Aavedan me apna naam, course, session aur roll number likhein aur batayein ki certificate kis kaam ke liye chahiye. College aapke record ke aadhar par certificate jaari karta hai.
  • Dhyaan rakhein — Character certificate aam taur par course poora hone ke baad ya aakhri saal me liya jata hai. Kai sansthan chhah mahine se zyada purana certificate nahi maante, isliye zaroorat padne par hi naya certificate lein.

3. Bonafide Certificate — kya hai aur kab chahiye

  • Kya hai — Bonafide Certificate is baat ka praman hai ki aap us college ke vartaman (current) student hain. Isme aapka naam, course, session aur college ka naam likha hota hai.
  • Kab chahiye — Scholarship ke aavedan, bank se education loan, passport, bus/train pass, ya kisi aur jagah "student hone" ka saboot dene ke liye bonafide certificate maanga jata hai.
  • Kahan se milta hai — Apne college ke office se. Ye TC se alag hai — bonafide certificate padhai ke dauraan milta hai, jabki TC course poora hone ke baad milta hai.
  • Kaise aavedan karein — College office me likhit aavedan dein aur batayein ki certificate kis kaam ke liye chahiye (jaise scholarship, bank loan ya passport). Aavedan ke saath aam taur par admission receipt aur ID proof ki copy lagti hai.
  • Dhyaan rakhein — Bonafide certificate par aam taur par us kaam ka naam likha hota hai, jiske liye woh liya gaya hai. Alag kaam ke liye alag certificate lena pad sakta hai, isliye aavedan me kaam saaf likhein.

Teeno certificate me antar — ek nazar me

  • TC — Course poora hone ya college chhodne par milta hai; doosre sansthan me admission ke liye chahiye.
  • Character Certificate — Aapke aacharan ka praman; admission aur naukri ke aavedan me maanga jata hai.
  • Bonafide Certificate — Vartaman student hone ka praman; scholarship, loan aur pass jaise kaam ke liye chahiye.
  • Migration Certificate — Ye in teeno se alag hai; woh university se milta hai jab aap doosri university me jate hain. Iske baare me alag guide me bataya gaya hai.

Aavedan se pehle ki checklist

  1. Sahi certificate chunein — Pehle dekhein ki aapko kis kaam ke liye kaun sa certificate chahiye. Galat certificate ke liye aavedan karne se samay barbaad hoga.
  2. Documents taiyaar rakhein — Admission receipt, college ID card, marksheet ki copy aur ek passport-size photo aam taur par kaam aate hain. College office se poochkar hi documents le jayein.
  3. Shulk aur samay college se poochhein — In certificate ke liye shulk aur taiyaar hone ka samay har college me alag hota hai. Aavedan dene se pehle office se ye dono baatein likhit ya saaf shabdon me pooch lein aur receipt sambhal kar rakhein.
  4. Naam aur details check karein — Certificate milte hi apna naam, course, session aur roll number dhyaan se padhein. Koi galti ho to turant office me batayein — baad me sudhar me zyada samay lagta hai.
  5. Copy sambhal kar rakhein — Har certificate ki photocopy aur scanned copy apne paas rakhein. Original sirf wahi dein, jahan original hi maanga gaya ho.
Zaroori baat: TC, Character Certificate aur Bonafide Certificate college se milte hain, university se nahi. University se sirf degree, provisional certificate, migration certificate aur transcript jaise kagazat milte hain. Agar koi vyakti in certificate ke naam par online paise maange ya "turant certificate dilane" ka dawa kare, to woh dhokha hai — hamesha apne college office se hi sampark karein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. TC aur Migration Certificate me kya farq hai?
A: TC college se milta hai aur batata hai ki aapne us college se padhai poori ki ya wahan se alag hue. Migration Certificate university se milta hai aur tab chahiye hota hai, jab aap Magadh University se doosri university me jate hain. Dono alag kagaz hain aur dono ki jagah alag hai.

Q2. Kya TC bina course poora kiye bhi mil sakta hai?
A: Haan, agar aap beech me college badal rahe hain to bhi TC ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain. Isme college chhodne ka kaaran likhna hota hai aur college ke niyam ke hisaab se TC jaari hota hai. Sahi prakriya apne college office se poochhein.

Q3. Character Certificate kitne samay tak valid rehta hai?
A: Is par koi ek niyam nahi hai — kai sansthan chhah mahine se zyada purana certificate nahi maante. Isliye certificate tabhi lein, jab aapko uski zaroorat ho, aur aavedan wali jagah ki shartein pehle padhein.

Q4. Kya ye certificate online bhi mil sakte hain?
A: Aam taur par ye certificate college office se hi milte hain aur in par principal ke hastakshar aur college ki muhar hoti hai. Kuch college online aavedan lete hain, lekin certificate college se hi lena hota hai. Apne college office se poochhein ki wahan kya prakriya hai.
