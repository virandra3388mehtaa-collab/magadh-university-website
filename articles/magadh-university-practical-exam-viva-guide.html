Magadh University Practical Exam Aur Viva Kaise Hota Hai — Poori Guide

Theory exam se pehle practical exam hota hai, aur practical ke saath hi viva-voce bhi hota hai — bahut se students ke liye yahi sabse zyada ghabrahat wala hissa hota hai, kyunki isme likhne se zyada karke dikhana aur bolkar samjhana padta hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki practical exam kab aur kahan hota hai, usme kya-kya hota hai, viva me kya poochha jata hai, kya lekar jayein aur taiyaari kaise karein. Is article me tareekhein sirf wahi likhi gayi hain jo university ki official jaankari ya is website par pehle se chhapi guide me di gayi hain — baaki baatein aam prakriya ki samajh ke liye hain, aur apne college se confirm karna hi aakhri hoga.

Practical Exam Kab Aur Kahan Hota Hai?

Practical exam theory exam se pehle hota hai aur apne-apne college me hota hai — jaise UG 7th Semester (session 2023-27) ke practical aur viva-voce 18 November 2026 se 23 November 2026 ke beech apne-apne college me ho rahe hain, aur theory exam 26 se 30 November 2026 ke beech hai. Har semester ka practical programme alag hota hai, aur practical ka schedule aur reporting time har college apna khud tay karta hai — isliye apna practical schedule aur samay apne college ke notice board ya department se pehle se confirm kar lein. Practical wale din late na pahunchein, kyunki practical ka batch aur samay pehle se tay hota hai.

Practical Exam Me Kya Hota Hai?

Practical exam me aapko apne subject ke prayog (experiment) karke dikhane hote hain, aur saath me aapki practical file ya record bhi dekha jata hai. Aam taur par practical exam me yeh hota hai — pehle aapko ek ya zyada prayog diye jate hain, jinhe aapko lab me karke unka parinaam (result) likhna hota hai; phir aapki practical file dekhi jati hai, jisme saal bhar ke kiye gaye prayog likhe hote hain; aur aakhir me viva-voce hota hai, jisme examiner aapse sawal poochhta hai. Science subjects me lab prayog, aur Commerce ya Arts ke practical subjects me unke apne tareeke se kaam hota hai — apne subject ke hisaab se teacher se poochh lein ki practical me kya-kya karna hoga.

Viva Me Kya Poochha Jata Hai?

Viva me zyadatar sawal unhi prayogon aur concepts se poochhe jate hain jo aapne practical me kiye hain — isliye practical ko ratne se zyada use samajhna kaam aata hai. Examiner aapse poochh sakta hai ki aapne yeh prayog kyun kiya, iska siddhant (principle) kya hai, parinaam aisa kyun aaya, aur file me likha hua kya matlab rakhta hai. Viva me lambi theory nahi poochhi jati — chhote aur seedhe sawal hote hain, aur jawab bhi chhota aur saaf hona chahiye. Agar kisi sawal ka jawab na aaye to imaandari se "pata nahi" kehna, galat jawab ghuma-phirakar dene se behtar hota hai. Ghabrahat kam karne ka sabse aasan tareeka hai ki practical se pehle apne doston se viva ki practice kar lein.

Practical Ke Liye Kya-Kya Lekar Jayein?

Practical wale din apna admit card, ek pehchan patra (ID card) aur apni poori practical file zaroor lekar jayein — aur jo bhi cheez aapka college ya department kahe, use bhi saath le jayein. Practical file me saare prayog saaf-suthre tareeke se likhe hon aur teacher ke hastakshar (signature) ho, to behtar hota hai — adhoori ya bina signature wali file se marks par asar pad sakta hai. Lab ke liye zaroori cheezein (jaise apron, calculator ya drawing ka saman, agar subject me lagta ho) bhi pehle se taiyaar rakhein. Exam se ek din pehle hi apna bag taiyaar kar lein, taaki subah kuch bhoolne ki ghabrahat na rahe.

Practical Ki Taiyaari Kaise Karein? — Aasan Tareeke

  1. Apni practical file poori karein — Saare prayog file me likhe hon, diagram aur parinaam saaf hon, aur teacher ke signature liye hon. Adhoori file viva me bhi pareshani deti hai.
  2. Har prayog ka siddhant samjhein — Sirf steps ratne se kaam nahi chalega; har prayog ka "kyun" samjhein, kyunki viva me wahi poochha jata hai.
  3. Apne doston se viva ki practice karein — Ek doosre se sawal poochhein aur jawab dein; isse bolne ki ghabrahat kam hoti hai aur jawab saaf hone lagta hai.
  4. College se schedule aur samay confirm karein — Practical ka din, batch aur reporting time pehle se jaan lein, taaki exam wale din daud-bhaag na ho.
  5. Exam form aur attendance ki shart poori rakhein — University ke ek official exam notice (memo CEO/1104/2026, dated 06/11/2026 — UG 3rd Semester 2025-29) me admit card ke liye 75% attendance aur internal exam pass ki shart likhi thi. Har semester ki shart uski apni notice me likhi hoti hai, isliye apne exam ki notice aur college office se confirm karein.

Practical Ke Marks Kaise Milte Hain?

Practical ke marks aam taur par internal aur external examiner, dono ke mulyankan se milte hain — practical me kiya gaya kaam, file aur viva, teeno ko dekha jata hai. Marks ka batwara (kitne marks practical ke, kitne file ke aur kitne viva ke) har subject aur course ke hisaab se alag hota hai, isliye apne subject ke marks pattern ke liye apne department se poochh lein. Ek udaharan — UG 7th Semester (2023-27) ke practical ke marks college portal par upload karega aur unki hard copy 05/12/2026 tak university ko bhejni hai, jo is website ki 7th semester guide me bhi bataya gaya hai. Apne marks ke baare me jaankari ke liye result aane par apne college ya student portal par dekhein.

Practical Me Fail Ya Absent Ho Gaye To Kya Hoga?

Practical me fail ya absent hone par turant apne college ke office ya department se baat karein — aage ka rasta (jaise back paper ya agle mauke ki prakriya) college aur university ki notice se hi tay hota hai. Practical me fail hone ka matlab yeh nahi hai ki saal kharab ho gaya — bahut se mamlo me back paper ya compartment exam ka rasta hota hai, jiski poori jaankari is website ki back paper guide me di gayi hai. Kisi bhi vyakti ke "paise dekar practical pass karwa doonga" ke dawe par bharosa na karein — aisa karna galat hai aur pakde jaane par badi pareshani ho sakti hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University me practical exam kab hota hai?
A: Practical exam theory exam se pehle aur apne-apne college me hota hai. Jaise UG 7th Semester (2023-27) ke practical aur viva 18 se 23 November 2026 ke beech ho rahe hain, aur theory 26 se 30 November 2026 ke beech hai. Har semester ka programme alag hota hai, isliye apne college se confirm karein.

Q2. Viva me kya poochha jata hai?
A: Viva me zyadatar sawal unhi prayogon aur concepts se poochhe jate hain jo aapne practical me kiye hain — prayog ka siddhant, parinaam aur file me likhi baatein. Chhote aur seedhe sawal hote hain, isliye practical ko samajhna hi sabse achhi taiyaari hai.

Q3. Practical exam me kya lekar jana chahiye?
A: Admit card, pehchan patra (ID card) aur poori practical file zaroor lekar jayein. Subject ke hisaab se lab ka saman (jaise apron ya calculator) bhi le jayein, aur college ne jo bhi cheez kahi ho, use bhi saath rakhein.

Q4. Practical me fail ho gaye to kya hoga?
A: Turant apne college se baat karein — aage ka rasta college aur university ki notice se tay hota hai, aur bahut se mamlo me back paper ya compartment exam ka rasta hota hai. Is website ki back paper guide me uski poori jaankari di gayi hai.

Q5. Practical ke marks kab aur kahan dikhenge?
A: Practical ke marks college university ko bhejta hai aur woh result ke saath dikhte hain. Jaise 7th semester ke practical ke marks ki hard copy 05/12/2026 tak university ko bhejni hai — apne result ke liye student portal ya college se jaankari lein.
