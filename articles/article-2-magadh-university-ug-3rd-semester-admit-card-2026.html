Magadh University UG 3rd Semester Admit Card 2026 — Download Kaise Karein, Session 2025-29

Magadh University UG 3rd Semester (B.A. / B.Sc. / B.Com., Session 2025-29) ki theory pariksha 26 November se 2 December 2026 tak hai (revised programme, memo CEO/1106/2026, 17 November 2026). 23 se 25 November ke beech kai students ke admit card jaari hote dikhe. Is guide me admit card paane ka tareeka, download ke steps, card par check karne wali cheezein aur 75% attendance ki shart di gayi hai.

Admit card kab aur kahan milega

Admit card university ke official student portal magadhonline.in par aane ki ummeed hai — U.G. 2025-29 section me. 23 November 2026 ki subah tak portal par link nahi dikh raha tha. Uske baad 23 se 25 November ke beech kai students ke admit card jaari hote dikhe. Portal par link na dikhe to apne college ke exam section se sampark karein. Admit card milte hi print nikaal lein.

Step by step: Admit card kaise download karein

  1. Official portal kholein: Browser me magadhonline.in kholein. Yahi university ka student portal hai.
  2. Apna section chunein: U.G. 2025-29 section me Semester-III ka admit card link kholein.
  3. Login karein: Wahi login details daalein jinse exam form bhara tha.
  4. Admit card kholein: Naam, roll number aur centre dhyan se milayein.
  5. Print nikaalein: PDF download karke kam se kam do print nikaal lein (A4, saaf print) — ek pariksha ke liye, ek backup ke liye.

Admit card par kya-kya check karein

Print nikaalne se pehle card par ye sab zaroor milayein:

  • Apna naam, pita ka naam, Registration Number aur Roll Number
  • Apna college ka naam aur exam centre ka naam aur pata
  • Chhe papers ki list — MJC-3 (26 Nov), MJC-4 (27 Nov), MIC-3 (28 Nov), MDC-3 (30 Nov), AEC-3 (1 Dec), SEC-3 (2 Dec) — apne subject ke hisab se
  • Har paper ka samay — Arts (Social Science/Humanities) subah 10:00–1:00, Science aur Commerce dopahar 2:00–5:00
  • Apni photo aur hastakshar card par saaf dikh rahe hon

75% attendance ki shart — dhyan dein

Memo CEO/1104/2026 (6 November 2026) ke anusaar pariksha me baithne ke liye 75% varg upasthiti (class attendance) aur internal pariksha me pass hona zaroori hai. Attendance kam ho ya internal me pass na hon to admit card me dikkat ho sakti hai — turant college ke exam section se baat karein.

Savdhaan — fraud se bachein: "Paise do, attendance kam hone par bhi admit card nikal denge" — ye poori tarah fraud hai. Admit card ki shart university ke memo me likhi hai, use koi dalal nahi badal sakta. Admit card ke liye kisi ko paise na dein aur apna login password ya OTP kisi ke saath share na karein.

Admit card me galti ho to kya karein

Agar card par naam, roll number, subject ya centre me koi galti dikhe, to turant apne college ke exam section se sampark karein. Poori jaankari hamare is article me di gayi hai: Admit Card me Galti Sudhaar Kaise Karwayein. Galti sudharwaye bina galat card lekar exam hall na jayein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. UG 3rd semester admit card 2026 kab aaya?
A: 23 se 25 November 2026 ke beech kai students ke admit card jaari hote dikhe. Portal aur college — dono jagah check karein.

Q2. Admit card kahan se download karein?
A: magadhonline.in ke U.G. 2025-29 section se, ya apne college ke exam section se.

Q3. Admit card download karne ke liye kya chahiye?
A: Wahi login details jinse exam form bhara tha.

Q4. Attendance kam hai to admit card milega?
A: Memo CEO/1104/2026 ke anusaar 75% attendance aur internal pass zaroori hai. Sahi jaankari college se milegi.
