Magadh University Me NSS Aur NCC Kaise Join Karein — Eligibility, Fayde Aur Aavedan Guide

College me padhai ke saath NSS (National Service Scheme) aur NCC (National Cadet Corps) do aise karyakram hain, jinme shamil hokar student samaj seva, anushasan aur netritva seekhte hain. Magadh University ke kai colleges me NSS aur NCC ki unit chalti hain, aur har saal naye students inme shamil hote hain. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki NSS aur NCC kya hote hain, kaun inhe join kar sakta hai, inke fayde kya hain aur aavedan kaise karein. NSS aur NCC ki unit har college me nahi hoti, isliye pehle apne college me unit hone ki jaankari confirm karein.

NSS Aur NCC Kya Hote Hain?

NSS (National Service Scheme) ek samaj-seva karyakram hai, jisme student swachhata abhiyan, jagrukta karyakram, swasthya shivir aur gaon-god lene jaise samajik kaam karte hain. Magadh University me NSS ki gatividhiyan chalti rehti hain — university ki official website par bhi NSS se judi khabrein dikhti hain. NCC (National Cadet Corps) ek anushasan aur prashikshan karyakram hai, jisme student drill, parade, shiviron aur netritva ke prashikshan me hissa lete hain. Dono hi karyakram padhai ke saath-saath vyaktitva vikas me madad karte hain.

NSS Aur NCC Kaun Join Kar Sakta Hai?

NSS aur NCC un colleges ke UG (snatak) students join kar sakte hain, jin colleges me NSS ya NCC ki unit chalti ho. Iske liye aam taur par ye shartein hoti hain:

  • College me unit hona — Sabse pehli shart ye hai ki aapke college me NSS ya NCC ki unit ho. Jin colleges me unit nahi hai, wahan ke student in karyakramon me shamil nahi ho sakte.
  • UG course me admission — Aam taur par snatak (UG) ke students in karyakramon ke liye aavedan karte hain. PG students ke liye niyam college ki unit par nirbhar karte hain.
  • Swasthya aur ruchi — NSS aur NCC dono me shaaririk gatividhiyan hoti hain, isliye student ko swasth hona chahiye aur samaj seva ya prashikshan me ruchi honi chahiye.
  • College ki chayan prakriya — Kuch colleges me seats seemit hoti hain, isliye unit ke incharge dwara chayan kiya jata hai. Sahi shartein aur prakriya apne college ki unit se hi poochhein.

NSS Aur NCC Join Karne Ke Fayde Kya Hain?

NSS aur NCC join karne ke fayde inme shamil hone par milte hain — koi bhi fayda "pakka" ya "guarantee" ke roop me nahi lena chahiye:

  • Certificate — Karyakram poora karne par student ko certificate milta hai, jo aage admission aur naukri ke aavedan me apni gatividhiyon ke saboot ke roop me dikhaya ja sakta hai.
  • Anushasan aur netritva — NCC me drill aur prashikshan se anushasan milta hai, NSS me samajik kaam se netritva aur team me kaam karne ki aadat banti hai.
  • Samajik kaam ka anubhav — NSS me gaon, swachhata aur jagrukta se jude kaam karke student samaj ko samajhta hai aur zimmedari nibhana seekhta hai.
  • Shiviron ka anubhav — Dono karyakramon me shiviron ka aayojan hota hai, jinme alag-alag colleges ke students se milne aur seekhne ka mauka milta hai.
  • Vyaktitva vikas — Stage par bolna, karyakram sambhalna aur logon se milna — ye sab aatm-vishwas badhata hai aur interview me bhi madad karta hai.

NSS Aur NCC Join Karne Ke Liye Aavedan Kaise Karein?

NSS ya NCC join karne ke liye ye steps apnayein:

  1. Apne college me unit check karein — Pehle jaanein ki aapke college me NSS ya NCC ki unit hai ya nahi. Ye jaankari college office ya notice board se mil jayegi.
  2. Unit ke incharge se milein — NSS ke liye Programme Officer aur NCC ke liye ANO (Associate NCC Officer) se milein aur enrolment ke baare me poochhein.
  3. Aavedan form bharein — Unit se aavedan form lein aur usme apna naam, course, session aur roll number saaf shabdon me bharein. Saath me college ID card aur ek photo taiyaar rakhein.
  4. Chayan prakriya poori karein — Agar seats seemit hain to unit dwara chayan kiya jata hai. Chayan ke baad aapka naam unit ki suchi me darj ho jata hai.
  5. Niyamit roop se hissa lein — Shamil hone ke baad unit ki baithkon, gatividhiyon aur shiviron me niyamit roop se hissa lena zaroori hota hai — bina sakriya hissa liye certificate nahi milta.

NSS Aur NCC Me Kya Antar Hai?

  • NSS — Samaj seva par kendrit hai: swachhata, jagrukta aur samajik kaam. Isme sena se juda prashikshan nahi hota.
  • NCC — Anushasan aur prashikshan par kendrit hai: drill, parade aur shivir. Isme sena se judi prashikshan gatividhiyan hoti hain.
  • Dono ka uddeshya — Dono hi student ke vyaktitva vikas ke liye hain, lekin tareeka alag hai. Kaun sa chunein, ye aapki ruchi par nirbhar karta hai.

NSS Aur NCC Join Karne Ki Last Date Kya Hai?

NSS ya NCC join karne ki koi ek university-level last date nahi hoti — har college ki unit apne samay par enrolment karti hai, aam taur par naye session ki shuruaat me. Sahi last date jaanne ke liye apne college ke notice board par dhyaan rakhein aur unit ke incharge se sampark karein. Kisi website ya vyakti dwara batayi gayi last date par bharosa karne se pehle use apne college se verify zaroor karein.

NSS Aur NCC Join Karne Ki Fees Kitni Hai?

NSS aur NCC me enrolment ke liye aam taur par koi badi fees nahi li jati, lekin shivir ya vardi se juda mamuli kharch ho sakta hai. Sahi jaankari apne college ki unit se hi lein aur jo bhi rakam dein, uski receipt sambhal kar rakhein. Koi vyakti NSS ya NCC me admission dilane ke naam par online paise maange to woh dhokha ho sakta hai — enrolment hamesha college ki unit se hi hota hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. NSS aur NCC kaun join kar sakta hai?
A: Un colleges ke UG students join kar sakte hain, jin colleges me NSS ya NCC ki unit chalti ho. Sahi shartein aur chayan prakriya apne college ki unit se poochhein.

Q2. NSS aur NCC join karne ki last date kya hai?
A: Iski koi ek university-level last date nahi hoti — har college ki unit apne samay par enrolment karti hai, aam taur par naye session ki shuruaat me. Sahi date apne college ke notice board ya unit ke incharge se poochhein.

Q3. NSS aur NCC me kaun sa chunein?
A: Agar aapko samaj seva, swachhata aur jagrukta ke kaam me ruchi hai to NSS behtar hai; agar anushasan, drill aur prashikshan me ruchi hai to NCC chunein. Dono hi vyaktitva vikas me madad karte hain.

Q4. Kya NSS ya NCC ka certificate naukri me kaam aata hai?
A: Certificate aapki gatividhiyon aur anubhav ka saboot hota hai aur aavedan me dikhaya ja sakta hai, lekin kisi bhi naukri ki guarantee nahi deta. Naukri ke liye yogya aur chayan prakriya hi tay hoti hai.
