Magadh University Convocation Aur Degree Certificate Kaise Lein — Original Degree Lene Ka Guide

Final year pass karne ke baad har student ko degree certificate ki zaroorat padti hai — naukri ke aavedan, aage ki padhai ya kisi bhi official kaam me. Kai students ko ye samajh nahi aata ki provisional certificate aur original degree me kya farak hai, degree kab aur kahan se milegi, aur uske liye kya karna hoga. Is guide me ye sab aasan shabdon me bataya gaya hai. Is article me degree ya convocation ki koi date ya fees nahi likhi gayi hai, kyunki ye har baar official notice se tay hoti hai — aakhri jaankari hamesha official notice ya apne college se hi lein.

Provisional certificate aur original degree me farak

  • Provisional certificate — ye asthayi praman patra hota hai jo result ke baad milta hai aur ye batata hai ki aapne course pass kar liya hai. Aage admission ya naukri ke aavedan me ye kaam aata hai, jab tak original degree na mile.
  • Original degree — ye university dwara diya gaya antim aur sthayi praman patra hota hai. Ye aam taur par convocation ke samay ya uske baad university/college ke dwara diya jata hai.
  • Migration certificate alag hota hai — migration certificate dusri university me admission ke liye chahiye hota hai; iska tareeka migration certificate guide me bataya gaya hai.

Original degree kaise milti hai

  • Convocation ke dwara — university samay-samay par convocation (deekshant samaroh) aayojit karti hai, jisme pass hue students ko degree di jati hai. Convocation kab hoga, iski jaankari official notice se milti hai.
  • College ke dwara — convocation ke baad ya kuch sthitiyon me degree college ke dwara bhi di ja sakti hai. Apne college ke office se poochhein ki aapki degree kab aur kahan se milegi.
  • Aavedan ki prakriya — degree ke liye aavedan kab, kis form se aur kin documents ke saath karna hai, ye har baar official notice se tay hota hai. Is article me koi form, fees ya last date nahi likhi gayi hai.
  • Online seva portal — university ke official seva portal (sportal.magadhuniversity.ac.in) par certificate se judi sevayein di jati hain; wahan se bhi jaankari le sakte hain.

Degree lene ke liye zaroori documents

  • Final year ki marksheet — apni antim varsh ki marksheet ki copy saath rakhein. Marksheet download karne ka tareeka marksheet guide me hai.
  • Registration card aur admit card — registration card aur final exam ka admit card saath rakhein, taaki aapki pehchan aur session ki pushti ho sake.
  • Pehchan patra — Aadhaar card ya koi aur sarkari pehchan patra saath rakhein.
  • Passport size photo — aavedan ke saath photo ki zaroorat pad sakti hai; college ya notice me di gayi jaankari ke hisaab se taiyaar rakhein.
  • Fees ki raseed — agar koi fees jama karni ho, to uski raseed sambhal kar rakhein. Fees ki rakam is article me nahi likhi gayi hai — sahi rakam official notice ya college se hi poochhein.

Degree lene se pehle ye check karein

  • Naam ki spelling — degree par aapka naam, pita ka naam aur janm-tithi wahi honi chahiye jo aapke registration card aur marksheet par hai. Galti dikhe to degree milne se pehle hi college ko batayein.
  • Course aur session — degree par course ka naam aur session sahi hai ya nahi, ye bhi check karein.
  • Galti ho to turant batayein — marksheet ya result me galti ho to kya karein, ye result ke baad wala guide me bataya gaya hai. Sudhaar ki prakriya college ke dwara hi aage badhti hai.

Convocation me kya hota hai

  • Degree vitran samaroh — convocation me university ke kuladhipati aur kulapati ki upasthiti me pass hue students ko degree di jati hai; kai baar toppers ko medal aur samman bhi diya jata hai.
  • Kaun shamil ho sakta hai — convocation me kaun se session ke students shamil honge, ye official notice me likha hota hai. Notice aane par apna session aur naam check karein.
  • Samay par jaankari lein — convocation ki date, sthan aur registration ki jaankari ke liye magadhuniversity.ac.in par notice dekhte rahein aur apne college se sampark me rahein.
Zaroori baat: Degree aur convocation ki date, fees aur aavedan ki prakriya har baar alag ho sakti hai. Is article me inme se koi bhi dawa nahi kiya gaya hai. Jo vyakti paise lekar degree jaldi dilwane ya naam badalwane ka vaada kare, usse savdhaan rahein — ye dhokha hota hai. Aakhri jaankari ke liye apne college ke office, magadhuniversity.ac.in ya official seva portal hi dekhein.

Degree milne ke baad kya karein

  • Degree ki copy sambhal kar rakhein — original degree aur uski kuch photocopy alag jagah rakhein; naukri ya admission ke aavedan me inki zaroorat padegi.
  • Aage ki padhai ya naukri — PG admission ke liye PG admission guide dekhein; Bihar sarkari naukri ki taiyaari ke liye sarkari naukri guide kaam aayega.
  • Scholarship ya anya kaam — degree milne ke baad bhi PMS scholarship jaise kaam ke liye marksheet aur degree ki copy chahiye hoti hai; steps PMS guide me hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Provisional certificate aur original degree me kya farak hai?
A: Provisional certificate result ke baad milne wala asthayi praman hai, jabki original degree university dwara diya gaya sthayi praman patra hai, jo aam taur par convocation ke samay ya uske baad milta hai.

Q2. Original degree kab milegi?
A: Iske liye har baar official notice aata hai — convocation ki date aur degree vitran ki prakriya notice se hi pata chalti hai. Is article me koi date nahi likhi gayi hai.

Q3. Degree ke liye kya documents chahiye?
A: Aam taur par final marksheet, registration card, admit card aur pehchan patra chahiye hote hain. Sahi list ke liye official notice ya apne college se confirm karein.

Q4. Degree par naam galat chhapa hai to kya karein?
A: Turant apne college ke exam office ko batayein aur marksheet aur registration card ki copy saath rakhein. Sudhaar ki prakriya college ke dwara hi aage badhti hai.
