Magadh University Admission Aur Exam Ke Liye Kaun Se Documents Chahiye — Poori List

Magadh University me admission lene, exam form bharne ya scholarship ke liye apply karne par aksar wahi documents mange jaate hain — lekin har kaam ki final list alag ho sakti hai. Is guide me woh saare documents ek jagah diye gaye hain jo aam taur par mange jaate hain, saath me har document ka kaam aur taiyaari ke tips. Final aur aakhri list hamesha usi kaam ke official notice ya form me di jaati hai — is article ko checklist ki tarah use karein, aakhri faisla college ke notice se hi karein. Isme koi date, fees ya eligibility ka dawa nahi kiya gaya hai.

Har kaam me lagbhag hamesha mange jaane wale documents

  • Aadhaar card — pehchan aur pate ka sabse aam proof. Naam aur janm-tithi dusre documents se milni chahiye.
  • Pichhli kaksha ki marksheet aur certificate — jis qualification ke basis par admission ya form bhar rahe hain, uski marksheet (jaise 10vi/12vi ya graduation).
  • Passport-size photo — haal ki saaf photo; form aur admit card dono me kaam aati hai. Kuch extra copy pehle se bana kar rakhein.
  • Admission receipt / admission slip — is saal ke course me admission ka proof; exam form aur scholarship dono me manga ja sakta hai.
  • Registration number / roll number ki jaankari — university se mila registration ya enrollment number form bharte samay chahiye hota hai.

Admission ke samay aam taur par mange jaane wale documents

  • Transfer certificate (TC) / School leaving certificate — pichhle school ya college se milta hai.
  • Migration certificate — agar pichhli padhai kisi dusre board ya university se ki hai, to ye manga ja sakta hai.
  • Character certificate — pichhle sansthan se mila aacharan praman-patra.
  • Caste certificate (agar lagu ho) — sarkari format me bana hua; reservation ya scholarship ke liye zaroori ho sakta hai.
  • Income certificate — parivar ki varshik aay ka praman; scholarship aur fee-chhoot se judi prakriya me manga ja sakta hai.
  • Residence (niwas) certificate — Bihar ka niwasi hone ka proof, agar manga jaye.
  • Domicile / EWS certificate (agar lagu ho) — jahan us category ka labh lena ho.

Exam form aur scholarship ke liye extra documents

  • Bank account details — account aapke apne naam par hona chahiye; scholarship me payment isi account me aati hai. Passbook ki copy ya cancelled cheque saath rakhein.
  • Bonafide certificate — college se milta hai; isse sabit hota hai ki aap us college ke chhatra hain.
  • Pichle semester ki marksheet — agle semester ke exam form ya scholarship renewal me mangi ja sakti hai.
  • Fee receipt — exam fees ya course fees jama karne ki raseed; form ke saath iski copy bhi mangi ja sakti hai.

Documents taiyaar rakhne ke 7 kaam ke tips

  1. Ek folder banayein: Saare original documents ek hi folder me rakhein aur unki photocopy alag lifafe me — zaroorat padne par dhoondhna na pade.
  2. Scan copy pehle se bana lein: Har document ki saaf scan copy ya achhi photo phone ya email me save karke rakhein. Online form bharte samay yehi kaam aati hai.
  3. Naam aur janm-tithi ek jaisi rakhein: Aadhaar, marksheet aur form me naam ki spelling aur janm-tithi ek jaisi honi chahiye. Farq dikhe to pehle college se sudhaar ki prakriya poochhein.
  4. Photocopy par sign aur date: Jahan self-attested copy mangi jaye, wahan photocopy par apne hastakshar aur date likhkar dein.
  5. Original kabhi kisi agent ko na dein: Original document sirf college office ya official counter par hi dikhayein; kisi bhi vyakti ko original dekar mat jayein.
  6. Expiry aur validity check karein: Income ya caste certificate purana ho to college ke niyam ke hisab se naya banwana pad sakta hai — form bharne se pehle validity poochh lein.
  7. Ek list bana kar tick karein: Form bharne se pehle is article ki list se apne documents milayein aur har document par tick lagayein — aakhri samay par kuch chhootne ka dar nahi rahega.
Zaroori baat: Koi bhi vyakti agar document banwane ya form bharne ke naam par paise mange, to pehle college office se jaanch lein. Certificate banwane ke liye official tareeka hi apnayein aur apne documents ki copy kisi anjaan vyakti ko mat dein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Kya ye saare documents har kaam me lagenge?
A: Nahi. Har kaam ki list alag hoti hai — admission, exam form aur scholarship me se har ek me inme se kuch documents lagenge. Final list usi kaam ke official notice ya form me milegi.

Q2. Caste ya income certificate kahan se banega?
A: Ye sarkari tareeke se bante hain — aam taur par RTPS/circle office ya online sarkari seva kendra se. Banwane ki prakriya aur samay ke liye apne jile ke official kendra ya college office se poochhein.

Q3. Document me naam ki spelling galat hai to kya karein?
A: Pehle college office se sudhaar ki prakriya poochhein aur sahi document banwayein. Galat spelling wala document form me use karne se aage pareshani ho sakti hai.

Q4. Original document kho gaya to kya karein?
A: Turant us document ko jaari karne wale office ya board se duplicate ke liye sampark karein. Duplicate milne tak uski photocopy aur FIR (agar zaroori ho) surakshit rakhein.
