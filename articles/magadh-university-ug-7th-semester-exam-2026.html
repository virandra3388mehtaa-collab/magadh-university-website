Magadh University UG 7th Semester Exam 2026 — Theory 26 Nov Se, Practical 18 Nov Se (Session 2023-27)

Session 2023-27 ke UG 7th Semester students ke liye pariksha ka karyakram jaari ho chuka hai. Theory pariksha 26 November 2026 se 30 November 2026 tak hogi aur practical pariksha 18 November 2026 se 23 November 2026 tak apne-apne college me hogi. Is guide me pariksha ki tareekhein, exam form ki last date, practical ki jaankari aur admit card se judi sahi baatein di gayi hain, taaki koi student kisi galat date ke chakkar me apna mauka na khoye.

7th Semester Ki Pariksha Kab Se Hai?

Theory pariksha 26 November 2026 se 30 November 2026 tak hogi. Practical pariksha 18 November 2026 se 23 November 2026 tak apne-apne college me hogi. Ye dates Controller of Examinations, Magadh University ke official notice (31 August 2026) ke anusar hain. Practical theory se pehle honge, isliye apne college se practical ki taiyaari pehle se shuru kar dein.

Kaun Se Students Ke Liye Hai?

Ye pariksha UG (CBCS) 7th Semester, session 2023–27 ke BA, BSc aur BCom students ke liye hai. Jo students is session me 7th semester me padh rahe hain, unhi ko is karyakram ke anusar theory aur practical pariksha deni hai.

Exam Form Ki Last Date Kya Hai?

Exam form bharne ki aakhri tareekh bina late fee ke 19 November 2026 hai. Form bharne ki prakriya 29 October 2026 se 19 November 2026 tak chalegi. Apply online official portal magadhonline.in aur magadhuniversity.ac.in se hota hai. Jin students ne abhi tak exam form nahi bhara hai, woh 19 November 2026 se pehle zaroor bhar dein — aakhri din ka intezaar na karein, kyunki aakhri samay me portal par bheed aur takneeki dikkat aam baat hai.

Practical Exam Me Kya Hoga?

Practical aur viva-voce 18–23 November 2026 ke beech apne-apne college me honge. Apne college se apna practical schedule aur reporting time pehle se confirm kar lein, kyunki har college apna samay-kram khud tay karta hai. Practical/viva ke marks college portal par upload karega aur unki hard copy 05/12/2026 tak university ko bhejni hai — isliye practical ke baad apne marks ki entry ki pushti college se kar lein.

Admit Card Kab Milega?

Admit card ki date is notice me nahi di gayi hai. Official website magadhuniversity.ac.in ya apne college se admit card ki suchna check karein — bina official suchna ke koi date na maanein. Admit card aate hi use samay par download karke usme apna naam, roll number aur exam centre dhyaan se check kar lein.

Direct Official Link

  • Magadh University ki official website: magadhuniversity.ac.in — notice aur jaankari ke liye.
  • Student portal (exam form, result, admit card): magadhonline.in
  • University service portal (certificate aur payment se judi sevayein): sportal.magadhuniversity.ac.in

Sahi jaankari ke liye hamesha college office aur university ki official website par hi bharosa karein.
