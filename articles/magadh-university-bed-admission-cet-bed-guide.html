Magadh University B.Ed Admission Kaise Lein — Bihar CET-B.Ed Exam, Eligibility Aur Counselling Guide

Teacher banna chahte hain aur Magadh University se B.Ed karna chahte hain? Bihar me B.Ed admission seedha college se nahi hota — iske liye ek state-level entrance exam dena padta hai, jiska naam hai Bihar B.Ed Common Entrance Test (CET-B.Ed). Ye exam Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Darbhanga Bihar sarkar ki taraf se karwati hai, aur isi exam ke rank ke aadhar par counselling se Bihar ke B.Ed colleges — jinme Magadh University ke antargat aane wale B.Ed colleges bhi shamil hain — me admission milta hai. Is guide me bataya gaya hai ki CET-B.Ed kya hai, kaun apply kar sakta hai, exam ka pattern kya hai aur counselling se admission kaise hota hai. Ye sab apne shabdon me likha gaya hai; dates, fees aur aakhri jaankari hamesha official website se hi lein.

Zaroori baat (2026 session): Bihar CET-B.Ed 2026 ki pariksha June 2026 me ho chuki hai aur uski counselling bhi chal chuki hai. Agle session (2027) ki dates abhi official taur par ghoshit nahi hui hain — is article me 2027 ki koi date nahi likhi gayi hai. Jaise hi official notice aayega, uski jaankari official website biharcetbed-lnmu.in par milegi. Neeche diya gaya exam pattern aur eligibility pichhle sessions par aadharit hai aur aam taur par har saal aisa hi rehta hai, lekin aakhri niyam hamesha naye notification se hi confirm karein.

B.Ed course aur Magadh University se kya lena-dena hai

  • B.Ed kya hai: Bachelor of Education — 2 saal ka regular course, jo teacher banne ke liye zaroori degree hai. Iske baad aap school teacher ki bharti (jaise Bihar STET ya CTET ke baad) ke liye apply kar sakte hain.
  • Magadh University me B.Ed: Magadh University ke antargat kai B.Ed colleges aate hain. In colleges me admission bhi Bihar CET-B.Ed ki counselling se hi hota hai — alag se Magadh University ka koi B.Ed entrance exam nahi hota.
  • Shiksha Shastri: CET-B.Ed se Shiksha Shastri course (Sanskrit medium B.Ed) me bhi admission hota hai — iske liye Sanskrit wali eligibility alag hoti hai (neeche dekhein).
  • Kitni seats: Bihar bhar me lagbhag 330 B.Ed colleges counselling me hissa lete hain — college chunne ke liye counselling me choice filling hoti hai, jisme aap apni pasand ke college chun sakte hain.
  • B.Ed course ke subjects, duration aur career options ki poori jaankari hamare B.Ed course page par hai.

Exam pattern — 120 sawal, 2 ghante, no negative marking

CET-B.Ed ek offline, pen-and-paper (OMR) exam hota hai. Ek sahi jawab par 1 mark milta hai aur galat jawab par koi negative marking nahi hoti — isliye har sawal ka jawab dena chahiye, khali chhodna aur galat jawab dena barabar hai.

  • Shiksha Shastri: isme General English ki jagah General Sanskrit Comprehension (15 marks) hota hai; baaki sections wahi rehte hain.

Aavedan se admission tak — poori prakriya

  1. Official notification padhein — official website biharcetbed-lnmu.in par notification aate hi use dhyaan se padhein. Dates, fees aur eligibility wahi se confirm karein.
  2. Online aavedan karein — official portal par jaakar form bharein: naam, janm tithi, category, graduation details aur pasand ke exam centre ki jaankari. Photo aur signature upload karein aur fees ka bhugtan online karein. Fees ki rakam notification me likhi hoti hai — is article me koi rakam nahi likhi gayi hai, kyunki ye har saal badal sakti hai.
  3. Admit card download karein — exam se kuch hafte pehle admit card official site par aata hai. Use download karke print kar lein aur us par exam centre, date aur samay check kar lein. Admit card download karne ka tareeka hamare admit card guide jaisa hi hota hai — registration number aur janm tithi se login karke.
  4. Exam dein — exam ke din admit card, photo ID aur neela/kaala ball pen lekar jayein. OMR sheet bharte samay roll number aur sawal ka number dhyaan se bharein.
  5. Answer key aur result — exam ke baad provisional answer key aati hai, phir final result ghoshit hota hai. Result me aapka rank aur qualifying status dikhta hai.
  6. Counselling ke liye register karein — result ke baad counselling portal par alag se registration karna padta hai. Isme aap apni pasand ke B.Ed colleges chunte hain (choice filling) — aap 12 tak college chun sakte hain.
  7. Seat allotment — aapke rank, college ki pasand aur reservation niyam ke hisaab se seat allot hoti hai. Allotment kai rounds me hota hai.
  8. College me reporting — seat milne par tay samay me college jana hota hai, documents dikhane hote hain aur admission fees jama karni hoti hai. Isi ke baad admission pakka hota hai.

Counselling aur college chunne me dhyaan dene wali baatein

  • Choice filling soch-samajh kar karein — college chunne se pehle us college ki fees, location, pichhle saal ki cutoff aur NAAC/NCTE manyata ke baare me jaankari lein. Sirf naam dekhkar college na chunein.
  • Magadh ke B.Ed colleges: Magadh University se jude B.Ed colleges me admission bhi isi counselling se hota hai. Counselling me college chunne ke samay Magadh ke colleges ki list bhi dikhegi — wahan se apni pasand ke hisaab se chunein.
  • Har round me dhyaan rakhein — counselling kai rounds me hoti hai. Pehle round me seat na mile to agle round ke liye wait karein; har round ki dates official portal par aati hain.
  • Documents pehle se taiyaar rakhein — counselling aur reporting ke samay ye documents chahiye hote hain: graduation ki marksheet aur degree, 10th aur 12th ke certificate, caste / EWS / PwD certificate (agar applicable ho), domicile certificate, photo ID, passport size photo aur CET-B.Ed ka admit card aur scorecard. Asli documents aur unki photocopy dono saath rakhein.
  • Fees sirf official tareeke se dein — admission fees hamesha college ya portal ke official tareeke se hi dein. Koi agent ya bicholiya seat dilwane ka vaada karke paise mange to usse savdhaan rahein — CET-B.Ed ki seat sirf rank aur counselling se hi milti hai.

Taiyaari kaise karein

  • General Awareness (40 marks) — sabse bada section hai: Bihar aur Bharat ki current affairs, itihas, bhugol, samvidhan aur shiksha se jude samachar par dhyaan dein. Roz newspaper ya current affairs notes padhein.
  • Reasoning (25 marks) — logical aur analytical reasoning ke sawal practice se aate hain; pichhle saal ke paper aur practice set hal karein.
  • Teaching-Learning Environment (25 marks) — school me padhai ka mahaul, shikshak ki bhoomika aur shiksha ke basic siddhant — ye section B.Ed ke liye khaas hai, isliye iski taiyaari alag se karein.
  • English aur Hindi (15+15 marks) — comprehension passage, vyakaran aur shabdawali par sawal aate hain; roz thoda padhne ki aadat banayein.
  • Pichhle saal ke paper: pichhle saal ke question paper se exam ka asli pattern samajh aata hai. Official site aur bharosemand sources se PYQ lein; kisi bhi paid ya leaked paper ke chakkar me na padein. PYQ se taiyaari ka tareeka hamare PYQ guide me hai.
  • Mock test: 2 ghante ka samay bandhkar poora paper hal karne ki practice karein — isse samay ka prabandhan aur OMR bharne ki aadat dono banenge.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Q1. Magadh University me B.Ed admission ke liye alag exam hota hai?
A: Nahi. Magadh University ke B.Ed colleges me admission Bihar CET-B.Ed exam aur uski counselling se hi hota hai. Magadh ka apna koi alag B.Ed entrance exam nahi hota.

Q2. CET-B.Ed me kitne marks chahiye hote hain?
A: Exam 120 marks ka hota hai aur counselling ke liye Unreserved category ko kam se kam 35% (42 marks) chahiye hota hai. Lekin college milna rank par depend karta hai — jitna accha rank, utna accha college.

Q3. CET-B.Ed me negative marking hoti hai?
A: Nahi. Galat jawab par koi number nahi kat-ta, isliye har sawal ka jawab dena chahiye.

Q4. Graduation me 50% se kam marks hain to kya apply kar sakte hain?
A: Aam category ke liye kam se kam 50% chahiye hota hai. SC / ST / OBC / EBC / PwBD / mahila candidates ke liye 45% chahiye hota hai. BE / B.Tech wale candidates ke liye 55% ka niyam hai.

Q5. 2027 session ke liye aavedan kab se hoga?
A: 2027 session ki dates abhi ghoshit nahi hui hain. Official website biharcetbed-lnmu.in par notification aate hi dates ki jaankari milegi — wahi se confirm karein, kisi aur source ki date par bharosa na karein.

Q6. B.Ed ke baad teacher ki naukri kaise milegi?
A: B.Ed ke baad aap CTET ya Bihar STET jaise teacher eligibility test de sakte hain, aur phir school teacher ki bharti ke liye apply kar sakte hain. B.Ed ke baad ke career options hamare B.Ed course page par diye gaye hain.
