About Us

Magadh University Info ek student-help website hai jo Magadh University, Bodh Gaya ke chhatron ke liye aasan bhasha me jaankari deti hai — result kaise check karein, admit card kaise download karein, exam form bharte samay kin galtiyon se bachein, scholarship ke liye kya documents chahiye, aur aise hi roz ke sawaalon ke jawab.

Is website ka maksad

University ke notice aksar PDF me aur angrezi me aate hain. Kai chhatra sirf isliye pareshan hote hain kyunki unhe sahi link, sahi tareekh ya sahi kadam samay par nahi milta. Is website ka ek hi maksad hai — official jaankari ko saral Hindi-mixed bhasha me, sahi kram me, ek jagah dena, taaki koi bhi chhatra cyber cafe ya dalal ke chakkar me na pade.

Content kaise banta hai

  • Har guide ki jaankari pehle Magadh University ki official website aur official notices se jaanchi jati hai.
  • Tareekh, fees ya eligibility jaisi baatein bina official saboot ke nahi likhi jateen.
  • Har page apne shabdon me likha jata hai — kisi doosri website ka content copy nahi kiya jata.
  • Galti dikhe to hum use sudharte hain; aap bhi Contact Us page se bata sakte hain.

Kaun chalata hai

Is website ko Rajan Kumar chalate hain — Bihar ke ek student aur content creator, jo khud pratiyogi parikshaon ki taiyaari aur chhatron ki madad ke kaam se jude hain. Website ke saath ek WhatsApp channel bhi hai jahan naye updates sabse pehle diye jate hain.

Kya ye University ki official website hai?

Nahi. Ye ek unofficial, swatantra student-help website hai. Iska Magadh University se koi prashasanik sambandh nahi hai. Admission, fees aur pariksha ke final faisle sirf University ke hote hain — isliye kisi bhi kadam se pehle official website magadhuniversity.ac.in par jaankari zaroor jaanch lein.
